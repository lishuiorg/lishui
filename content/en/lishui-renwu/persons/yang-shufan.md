Yang Shufan is one of two national-level representative bearers of the Luoshan Big Dragon known to this library. This entry covers only the man; the history, form and origin tradition of the dragon belong to the entry on the project and are not repeated here.

## Register identity

His bearer page on the China Intangible Cultural Heritage Network gives: number 03-0903, male, born May 1936; a third-batch national-level representative bearer of the project 舞龙 (Luoshan Big Dragon); applying region or unit "Lishui County, Jiangsu"; and records his death in April 2009.

"Lishui County" is quoted as written, at a time before Lishui became a district. The project name on this page reads 舞龙, while the project page this library holds reads 龙舞; both are quoted as written and not harmonised. A bearer's batch and a project's batch are different things: the Luoshan Big Dragon project itself entered the second national batch in 2008, while Yang Shufan belongs to the third batch of national-level representative bearers.

## Place in the tradition

The résumé on the bearer page for Yang Muhai records that in 2007 he "assisted the work of Yang Shufan, former president of the Luoshan Big Dragon association and national-level bearer, and mastered the full set of procedures for taking the dragon out". On this basis Yang Shufan was described as president of the association by 2007 at the latest, and was still carrying out transmission work then.

The nature of the source matters: the sentence comes from a résumé on another bearer's page and is that page's own account, not an independent record. What the word "former" covers, and the nature and duration of the association itself, are not recorded in this library's sources.

## Relation to Lishui

The page gives an applying region or unit, "Lishui County, Jiangsu". An applying region is the register's administrative framing, not a birthplace; this library's sources record neither his native place nor his birthplace, so the relation is recorded as activity rather than birth. The village where the dragon is transmitted, Luoshan, has its own entry in the towns station.

## Points of doubt

First, birth and death are known only to the precision "born May 1936" and "died April 2009", both from the same page. This library has not verified them against a printed edition or a second source; the page changes over time, so the access date (2026-09-26) must be cited.

Second, the applying region "Lishui County, Jiangsu" does not establish a birthplace; his family and how he learned the tradition are not recorded.

Third, the office of president of the Luoshan Big Dragon association appears only in the résumé on Yang Muhai's page, which is self-reported. The association's founding, structure and terms of office are not recorded in this library's sources.

Fourth, when he began working with the dragon, in what manner he transmitted it, and to whom, are recorded in none of this library's sources. The phrase "fourth-generation inheritor" appears only on Yang Muhai's page and refers to Yang Muhai; it must not be transferred to Yang Shufan.

Fifth, this library holds no district-level register entry for him, so his local standing before the national designation cannot be judged.
