Madam Mei is one of the chaste widows of Lishui recorded in juan 7, the "Chaste and Martyred Women" biography, of the Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*. The gazetteer places her and her daughter-in-law Madam Zheng in a single item, Madam Mei first and Madam Zheng appended; this entry treats Madam Mei alone, and Madam Zheng has an entry that links back to this one. The gazetteer gives only her natal surname Mei and her husband Xu Yiwei of the city west. The passage sits under a heading "Sojourners"; this library judges that word a suspect heading, as explained in its own section below. This entry rests on a single item in this gazetteer and adds nothing the text does not record.

## The gazetteer text

> 梅氏，城西許一唯妻。年廿四守志撫孤，八十五歲而卒。署縣令程萬里以「節儉雙美」獎之。媳鄭氏，昌佐妻，廿五歲守節撫孤，事姑孝，年已七旬矣。
> —— 康熙《溧水县志》卷之七·贞烈传

## Reading

The item contains Madam Mei and her daughter-in-law Madam Zheng; this entry reads Madam Mei alone.

"Madam Mei, wife of Xu Yiwei of the city west" gives her natal surname, her residence, and her husband's name. "City west" is the residence the gazetteer records, a rare specific location in this volume; her husband's name is kept as written. Her given name, courtesy name, and paternal family are unrecorded.

"From twenty-four she kept her resolve and raised an orphan, and died at eighty-five" gives the start, the substance, and the end: at twenty-four she resolved to keep her chastity and raised the orphan; she died at eighty-five. These are the gazetteer's ages and record; her son's name, the year of her marriage, and her husband's years are unrecorded.

"The acting magistrate Cheng Wanli honoured her with the words 'Chaste and thrifty, a double excellence'" gives the honouring official and the plaque text. "Acting magistrate" means one holding the county office in an acting capacity, here Cheng Wanli; the plaque bears the words "Chaste and thrifty, a double excellence", a rare text in this volume.

"Her daughter-in-law Madam Zheng, wife of Changzuo, kept her chastity and raised an orphan from twenty-five, served her mother-in-law with filial piety, and is now seventy" is appended matter on Madam Zheng, who is Xu Changzuo's wife. This layer belongs to Madam Zheng and has its own entry (`ls:person:zheng-shi-xuchangzuo`), to which this one links.

## The suspect heading "Sojourners"

The passage this item belongs to carries a heading "Sojourners" in the transcription. This library judges the word a suspect heading: what follows (Cheng Tianmin's daughter, the Chen daughter, Wu Ai, Madam Ma, and others) are still chaste widows and martyred women, and does not match the sense of "sojourners". This library keeps the word as recorded and marks it a suspect heading, and does not read this item as belonging to a sojourner biography; whether the passage continues the Chaste and Martyred Women biography or is another biography must be settled against the printed edition.

## Relation to Lishui

This entry enters her as "active in Lishui", and the reason must be stated. What the gazetteer records is her chastity in Lishui: living in the city west, wife of Xu Yiwei, keeping her resolve and raising an orphan from twenty-four, dying at eighty-five, and honoured by the acting magistrate Cheng Wanli with "Chaste and thrifty, a double excellence"; her residence is given as the city west, and her daughter-in-law Madam Zheng served her. But the gazetteer records no birthplace or native place for her, naming her only "Madam Mei", so this entry does not record her as "born locally". There is no office, recommendation, or writing to attach to her, so "official service", "recommended from Lishui", and "writings" do not apply; since the record is of keeping her home, not of lodging abroad, "sojourn" does not apply either. Of the five categories only "active in Lishui" holds, and this entry uses it.

## Points of doubt

First, her birth and death years are unrecorded. The gazetteer gives only that she kept her resolve from twenty-four and died at eighty-five; it gives no year of birth, of marriage, or of her husband, and this entry converts and infers nothing.

Second, her birthplace and native place are not explicitly recorded. The gazetteer calls her only the wife of Xu Yiwei of the city west and "Madam Mei"; her paternal family and native place are unrecorded, and "city west" is a residence, not a native place.

Third, beyond her husband's name, her paternal family and offspring are not fully recorded. The gazetteer gives her husband Xu Yiwei, her child (the orphan), and her daughter-in-law Madam Zheng; her son's name and later career are unrecorded.

Fourth, only the honouring official and the plaque text are recorded, not the year of the honour. The gazetteer says only that the acting magistrate Cheng Wanli honoured her with "Chaste and thrifty, a double excellence".

Fifth, her daughter-in-law Madam Zheng has her own entry (`ls:person:zheng-shi-xuchangzuo`), to which this one links; the two share a single item, and separate entries merely give each her due.

Sixth, this entry rests on a single item in this gazetteer, in the "Sojourners" (suspect heading) block of the Chaste and Martyred Women biography; the volume is an online transcription, not checked against a punctuated edition or the printed text, so citations must be marked as from the transcription.

Seventh, searches across the other source layers of this library produced nothing that could add to her.
