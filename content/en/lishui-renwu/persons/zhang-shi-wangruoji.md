Madam Zhang is one of the chaste widows recorded in the "Guochao" section of the Biographies of Chaste and Martyred Women in juan 7 of the Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*. The gazetteer gives only her surname, Zhang, and her husband's name, Wang Ruoji. The section is headed "Guochao" (the present dynasty) in the original, that is, the early Qing, the age in which the compiler lived; this library renders it as the Qing period and notes the gazetteer's wording, so the `birth_era` of this entry is "Qing", which takes the gazetteer's dynastic grouping and is not a record of birth. This entry rests on no more than this single item in juan 7, and supplies nothing the gazetteer does not record.

## Text of the gazetteer

> 章氏，王若驥妻。年二十餘夫死，守節五十三年。署縣事程萬里旌獎之。
> —— 康熙《溧水县志》卷之七·贞烈传

## Reading

The item records three things.

"Madam Zhang, wife of Wang Ruoji" gives her surname and her husband's name. Her personal name, courtesy name, native place and paternal kin are nowhere recorded, and this entry supplies none of them.

"Her husband died when she was in her early twenties" is the gazetteer's record: her husband Wang Ruoji died when she was in her twenties. "In her twenties" is the gazetteer's approximate age for her widowhood; this entry does not change it into an exact figure, nor infer her birth year from it.

"She kept her chastity for fifty-three years" is the length of widowhood the gazetteer gives, and is recorded as such. No start or end year is attached; reckoning from a widowhood beginning in her twenties, she would have died in her seventies, but the gazetteer records neither her death year nor her age at death, and this entry does not make that reckoning, quoting only the number of years.

"Cheng Wanli, acting administrator of the county, conferred honours on her" is the gazetteer's record of honours. The one who honoured her is "Cheng Wanli, acting administrator of the county"; "acting administrator of the county" is the office title the gazetteer gives, meaning one deputising in the county administration, and this entry quotes that title as it stands without changing it to "magistrate". "Cheng Wanli" is his name, quoted as given; this entry does not supply his courtesy name, career or years of tenure from elsewhere. The gazetteer records no year for the honours and no inscription.

## Relation to Lishui

The `relation` field is "Active in Lishui", and the reason must be stated. What the gazetteer records is this woman's conduct of chastity at Lishui — widowed young, chaste for fifty-three years, and honoured by the acting administrator of the county; the honours issued from the acting official of this county, and her village must have lain within Lishui, so her conduct has to do with Lishui. Yet the gazetteer never records her birthplace or native place, calling her only by her surname Zhang, so "native of Lishui" is not filled; and it has no office, no recommendation and no writings on which "official service", "recommended from Lishui" or "writings" could rest; what it records is her keeping widowhood at home, not sojourning elsewhere, so "sojourn" does not apply either. Of the five values, only "active in Lishui" can be grounded, and on that this entry enters the library.

## Points of doubt

First, her birth and death years are unrecorded. The gazetteer gives only the two ends, that her husband died in her twenties and that she was chaste for fifty-three years, without her birth or death year, and this entry infers neither.

Second, her birthplace and native place are not expressly recorded. The gazetteer calls her only "Madam Zhang" and records neither her paternal kin nor her residence; this entry does not infer her native place from her husband's surname or from other items in this volume.

Third, apart from her husband's name Wang Ruoji, her paternal kin and her offspring may not be fully recorded. This item records no children, and no parents or brothers.

Fourth, the honours may not be complete. The gazetteer gives only the one sentence that Cheng Wanli, acting administrator of the county, conferred honours on her, with no year and no inscription, and it does not say whether the honour was for chastity, for chastity and filial piety, or under another name; this entry quotes it as it stands, does not supplement it from other items in this volume, and does not supplement the person of Cheng Wanli from other entries in this library.

Fifth, this entry rests on one item of this gazetteer alone. It is taken from the "Guochao" section of the Biographies of Chaste and Martyred Women in juan 7, an online transcription not checked against a punctuated edition or the printed book, so all quotations are marked "from the online transcription".

Sixth, this library has searched its other sources and found no material that would supplement this person.
