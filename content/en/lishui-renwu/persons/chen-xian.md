Chen Xian is one of the Ming officials of Lishui listed in juan 6 of the Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*. His biography is extremely short: one line of origin, one year of appointment, two sentences of praise, one sentence about a gazetteer. This entry records exactly that, and sets the gazetteer sentence side by side with this library's existing sequence of Lishui editions without letting that sequence say things this volume does not say.

## Name and origin

> 陈宪，字伯度，余干人。由进士，正德八年任。
> — Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, juan 6

He was courtesy-named Bodu, a native of Yuqan, entered office through the *jinshi* degree, and was appointed to Lishui in the eighth year of the Zhengde era. That year is a definite year given by this volume, and it gives only the year of arrival.

## What the record says about his Lishui term

> 公明宽恕，操洁廉平。
> — Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, juan 6

Both sentences are the gazetteer's judgement: fair and clear in public business, generous to others, clean and even in personal conduct. The biography records no measure, building, case or fiscal act during his term, and this entry infers none from the praise.

## The gazetteer he compiled

> 曾修溧水志。
> — Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, juan 6

This single sentence is the entire gazetteer basis this library has for linking Chen Xian to Lishui's older county gazetteers. This volume says only that he "once compiled" one: no year, no fellow compilers, no number of juan, no arrangement, no statement of whether it survives.

The entry "Five Extant Editions of the Lishui County Gazetteer" in this library records a sequence in which the Lishui gazetteer was first compiled in the fourth year of the Zhengde era by Chen Xian and Fan Qi, and that no copy of it survives; a separate entry in this library records that Wang Congshan's biography likewise credits him with compiling the county gazetteer. These can be read against the present biography — the "once compiled" here corresponds to the first compilation in that sequence. But the boundary must hold: this biography gives no year of compilation, so this entry does not use the sequence to supply what the biography does not record (the year, the fellow compiler Fan Qi, the arrangement and the juan count are not in this volume); and this biography says nothing about whether that gazetteer survives, and this entry adds nothing from outside the sequence either.

## Relation to Lishui

He was not a Lishui native and enters this library through official service. Two sentences tie him to the county: his appointment, and the statement that he compiled its gazetteer. The second is the only sentence in the biography that names a concrete matter.

## Points of doubt

First, his birth and death years are not recorded; his native place is given only as Yuqan.

Second, the year he left Lishui is not recorded; only the year of arrival is given.

Third, the year, juan count, arrangement, fellow compilers and survival of the gazetteer he compiled are nowhere recorded in this volume.

Fourth, the dates do not agree, and this entry keeps both without choosing. This biography places his appointment in the eighth year of the Zhengde era; this library's sequence of editions places the first compilation in the fourth year of the Zhengde era. This library holds no material that decides which is right, and this entry uses neither to fix his year of arrival or his year of compiling.

Fifth, the praise is the gazetteer's judgement, and the deeds behind it are not recorded.

Sixth, his later career, final rank and place of death are not recorded.

Seventh, this entry rests on one biography in this volume. The sequence of editions belongs to another entry of this library, and that sequence itself rests on bibliographic description; this entry does not treat it as gazetteer text from this volume.
