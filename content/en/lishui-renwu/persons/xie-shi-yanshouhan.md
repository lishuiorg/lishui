Madam Xie is one of the Ming-era chaste women of Lishui in the "Biographies of the Chaste" of juan 7 in the Kangxi Lishui County Gazetteer, recorded as the wife of Yan Shouhan. Her notice is distinctive for spanning three generations of orphan-rearing. This entry rests on that single record.

## Gazetteer text

> 謝氏，顏守翰妻，年二十二夫死，撫子；子死，撫孫；孫死，撫曾孫，苦節六十年。崇禎間表其門曰「貞節」。媳李氏，孫媳蔣氏，皆青年守節。
> —— 康熙《溧水县志》卷之七·贞烈传

## Reading of the text

First, "wife of Yan Shouhan, widowed when she was twenty-two" dates her widowhood.

Second, "she raised her son; when the son died she raised her grandson; when the grandson died she raised her great-grandson, keeping a bitter chastity for sixty years." This sequence across three generations is reproduced as the gazetteer gives it.

Third, "in the Chongzhen era her gate was honoured as 'Chaste.'" The reign era is kept as given and not converted; the conferring authority is not named.

Fourth, "her daughter-in-law Li-shi and her grandson's wife Jiang-shi both kept their chastity from youth" is a supplementary note. The gazetteer gives no further detail on the two, and this entry does not create separate notices for them.

## Relation to Lishui

The gazetteer identifies her only through her husband, Yan Shouhan, whose household was her place of residence; her three generations of orphan-rearing and the honour to her gate attach to the Lishui countryside. The text neither states her birthplace nor calls her a native of Lishui, so this entry does not mark her as "native of Lishui." She held no office, received no recommendation and left no writings, so the association is recorded as "active in Lishui," based on her conduct recorded there.

## Points of doubt

First, no birth or death years are given; "twenty-two" and "sixty years" are the gazetteer's figures.

Second, her birthplace and native place are not stated; the gazetteer ties her only to her husband's name.

Third, the life, rank and death year of Yan Shouhan are unrecorded, and the names and genealogy of her son, grandson and great-grandson are not given.

Fourth, the daughter-in-law Li-shi and the grandson's wife Jiang-shi are only noted as keeping chastity, with no further detail, and are not given separate entries here.

Fifth, "in the Chongzhen era" dates only the honour; no conferring authority, number or tablet inscription is given, and "Chaste" is the gate-name the gazetteer records.

Sixth, this entry rests on one notice in juan 7 of the Kangxi gazetteer, a network transcription not checked against a critical or printed edition.
