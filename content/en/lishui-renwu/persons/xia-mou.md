Xia Mou is one of the seven men in the "Song" block of the Filial Sons biography in juan 7 of the Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*. His line is a single sentence: a surname, the statement that his name is lost, a residence, one clause on his care for his mother, and the year and name of a commendation. The biography records no birth or death years and no family, and this entry writes only what this line records, supplying neither a name nor a life.

## Where the gazetteer places him

The gazetteer places him as "a man of Qiqiao", one of the residences the biography records: he lived at Qiqiao, and the gazetteer records neither which township he belonged to, nor where his house stood, nor where he was born. This entry records the residence and supplies no township and no address.

## What the gazetteer records, and the commendation

> 事母至孝，淳熙間旌其里曰「昭孝」。
> — Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, juan 7

"In the Chunxi era" is the year of the commendation as the gazetteer gives it; this entry reproduces it without converting it to a Common-Era year and infers from it neither his birth and death nor the year of his conduct. "Bright Filiality" is the commendatory name of his ward as the gazetteer words it, and this entry reproduces the two characters.

## The doubt about the name "Bright Filiality"

This line records that his ward was commended under the name "Bright Filiality". The task brief states that the Chaste and Exemplary Women biography of the same gazetteer contains a second passage, "in the Chunxi era it commended its ward under the name 'Bright Filiality'", which should be recorded side by side with no decision on whether the two are the same ward. Under a full search of juan 7 in the source card this library actually reads, the two characters for "Bright Filiality" appear in only this one place: the Chaste and Exemplary Women part of that card carries only a summary of categories, not the gazetteer text, and the summary does not contain this phrase. This entry therefore quotes no passage this library has not obtained, and states three points: first, in the material this library holds, "Bright Filiality" occurs only here; second, if the other biography does carry such a passage, this library has not obtained its text and there is nothing to compare side by side until the gazetteer text of that biography is read; third, in either case, whether the two refer to one ward and whether the two commendations are one event cannot be decided from this library's material.
