Madam You is one of the figures in the Yuan section of the Chaste and Staunch biography in juan 7 of the Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*. The notice records her husband, her vow bitten in blood after his death, and the honouring of her gate by the authorities. This entry keeps those layers apart and supplies no name, home place or date for her.

## The gazetteer text

> 尤氏，曹子英妻。年少夫死，齧指自誓，終不貳。有司以聞，旌其門。
> — Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, juan 7, Chaste and Staunch biography

## Her station and her husband's house

"Madam You" is the designation the gazetteer gives, with only a surname and no personal or courtesy name, so this entry keeps the gazetteer's "Madam You" as its title. "Wife of Cao Ziying" is the husband the gazetteer records, whose name is Cao Ziying, reproduced here without alteration. The gazetteer gives neither her home place nor the places of her paternal or marital house, and no residence or native place is supplied.

## Her husband's death and her bitten vow

"Widowed young" is the gazetteer's account of her lot: her husband died while she was still young. The year of his death and the year of their marriage are unrecorded. "She bit her finger in a vow and to the end did not take a second husband" is the gazetteer's account of her chastity: she bit her finger to make her vow, and never remarried to the end. The words are the gazetteer's own and are reproduced as its account; her vow and the number of years she kept her chastity are not recorded.

## The honouring of her gate by the authorities

"The authorities reported her, and her gate was honoured" is the gazetteer's account of what followed: the authorities reported her deed upwards, and her gate was honoured. In what year this was done, on whose memorial, and under what title are wholly unrecorded, and nothing is inferred here from other juan. This is the notice's account of the honour she received and belongs to the gazetteer.

## Relation to Lishui

The gazetteer records her as the wife of Cao Ziying, and records her vow after her husband's death and the honouring of her gate. She enters this site with the relation "active in Lishui", and the reasoning must be stated: the gazetteer records no birth place, native place or township, so "born in Lishui" does not apply; it records no office she held in Lishui, no writings of hers and no recommendation of her out of Lishui, so "official service", "writings" and "recommended from Lishui" do not apply; and the biography belongs to the "Chaste and Staunch" section of this county's gazetteer and records her chastity and the honour she received, which this library treats as her activity in Lishui, so the relation taken is "active in Lishui". The limits must be stated: the gazetteer records no home place for her, so "active in Lishui" here rests only on her appearing in this county's Chaste and Staunch biography and carries no explicit statement that she acted in Lishui.

## Points of doubt

First, her birth and death years are not recorded, nor the year of her husband's death nor the year of their marriage; nothing is converted and nothing inferred.

Second, neither her personal nor her courtesy name is recorded, and the entry stands under the gazetteer's own form, Madam You.

Third, her birth place and native place are not explicitly recorded: the gazetteer gives only that she was the wife of Cao Ziying and no home place, so no native place can be decided.

Fourth, the life of her husband Cao Ziying is unrecorded, and Cao Ziying has no entry in this library.

Fifth, the words of her bitten vow and the number of years she kept her chastity are unrecorded.

Sixth, the year, memorial and title of the honouring of her gate are unrecorded, and nothing is inferred here from other juan.

Seventh, this entry rests on a single notice in the Chaste and Staunch biography of juan 7, and this library has nothing further to add; the volume used here is an online transcription, not checked against a punctuated edition or the printed text, so citations must be marked as from the transcription.
