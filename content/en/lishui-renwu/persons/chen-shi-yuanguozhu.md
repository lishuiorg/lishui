Madam Chen is one of the chaste widows of Lishui recorded in juan 7, the "Chaste and Martyred Women" biography, of the Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*. The gazetteer gives only her natal surname Chen, her husband Yuan Guozhu of Bailu Township, and three things: her chastity, her teaching of her son, and an honour. The passage sits under a heading "Sojourners"; this library judges that word a suspect heading, as explained in its own section below. This entry rests on a single item in this gazetteer and adds nothing the text does not record.

## The gazetteer text

> 陳氏，白鹿鄉袁國柱之妻。年十八夫死，矢志撫孤，備嘗辛苦。工織紝女工，暇則教子，慈母猶如嚴父。縣令朱英旌獎之「苦節永貞」。
> —— 康熙《溧水县志》卷之七·贞烈传

## Reading

The item is layered.

"Madam Chen, wife of Yuan Guozhu of Bailu Township" gives her natal surname, her place, and her husband's name. Bailu Township is one of the township names this volume records for Lishui, kept as written; her husband's name is kept as written.

"Widowed at eighteen, she vowed to raise her orphan and endured great hardship" gives the start and substance of her widowhood: her husband died when she was eighteen; she vowed to raise the orphan; she endured great hardship. Her vow and her raising of the child are recorded; her son's name and the number of years of her chastity are unrecorded.

"Skilled at weaving and needlework, she taught her son in her spare time, a loving mother like a stern father" gives her household skill and teaching: skilled at weaving and needlework, teaching her son in her spare time, a loving mother who was like a stern father. Her skill and diligence in teaching are recorded, and the phrase "a loving mother like a stern father" is the gazetteer's judgement of her combining tenderness and severity.

"The magistrate Zhu Ying honoured her with the words 'Bitter chastity, everlasting purity'" gives the honouring official and the plaque text, a rare text in this volume. The year of the honour is unrecorded.

## The suspect heading "Sojourners"

The passage this item belongs to carries a heading "Sojourners" in the transcription. This library judges the word a suspect heading: what follows (Cheng Tianmin's daughter, the Chen daughter, Wu Ai, Madam Ma, and others) are still chaste widows and martyred women, and does not match the sense of "sojourners". This library keeps the word as recorded and marks it a suspect heading, and does not read this item as belonging to a sojourner biography; whether the passage continues the Chaste and Martyred Women biography or is another biography must be settled against the printed edition.

## Relation to Lishui

This entry enters her as "active in Lishui", and the reason must be stated. What the gazetteer records is her chastity in Lishui: wife of Yuan Guozhu of Bailu Township, keeping chastity from eighteen and teaching her son as both loving and stern, finally honoured with "Bitter chastity, everlasting purity" by the county magistrate Zhu Ying — her community her home and the honouring official a Lishui officer. But the gazetteer records no birthplace or native place for her, naming her only "Madam Chen", so this entry does not record her as "born locally". There is no office, recommendation, or writing to attach to her, so "official service", "recommended from Lishui", and "writings" do not apply; since the record is of keeping her home, not of lodging abroad, "sojourn" does not apply either. Of the five categories only "active in Lishui" holds, and this entry uses it.

## Points of doubt

First, her birth and death years are unrecorded. The gazetteer gives only that she was widowed at eighteen; it gives no year of birth, no year for her husband, no number of years of chastity, and no year of death, and this entry converts and infers nothing.

Second, her birthplace and native place are not explicitly recorded. The gazetteer calls her only "Madam Chen"; her paternal family and residence are unrecorded, and this entry does not infer a native place from her husband's township.

Third, beyond her husband's name, her paternal family and offspring are not fully recorded. The gazetteer gives only her husband Yuan Guozhu; his courtesy name and status are unrecorded, as are the orphan's name and later career.

Fourth, only the honouring official and the plaque text are recorded, not the year of the honour.

Fifth, this entry rests on a single item in this gazetteer, in the "Sojourners" (suspect heading) block of the Chaste and Martyred Women biography; the volume is an online transcription, not checked against a punctuated edition or the printed text, so citations must be marked as from the transcription.

Sixth, searches across the other source layers of this library produced nothing that could add to her.
