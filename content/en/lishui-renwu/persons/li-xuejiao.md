Li Xuejiao is the only person among this group of entries whom both Lishui District documents record. Both documents label themselves the third batch, yet the transmitter lists they attach are different, so this entry reports what each one records instead of merging them into a single statement.

## Registry status

**Li Zheng Fa [2017] No. 197 (2017).** The notice of the People's Government of Lishui District on publishing the third batch of district-level protected sites, district-level intangible cultural heritage projects and district-level transmitters is dated 22 November 2017 and was posted on the district government website in October 2018; the batch year follows the year carried by the document number. Its Annex 3 is headed "List of representative transmitters (19 persons in total)". This library verified 14 name entries in it, among them 李雪皎（溧水剪纸）, that is the name followed by the project name in parentheses. The annex gives name and project only, with no birth date, no project category and no address.

**Li Zheng Ban Fa [2021] No. 3 (2021).** The notice of the General Office of the People's Government of Lishui District on publishing the third batch of district-level representative projects and representative transmitters is dated 11 January 2021. Its Annex 2 is headed "Third batch of district-level intangible cultural heritage representative transmitters (9 persons in total)". Row 1 is 李雪皎, and its columns read, transcribed exactly as written — birth month 1965年12月, project name 溧水剪纸, project category 传统美术 (traditional visual arts), unit or address 永阳街道通济街社区.

The two documents differ in issuing body, the district government against its own general office, in the size of the list, 19 against 9, and in their columns. The source cards state that the two must not be merged and that neither may be used to decide which list is the formal one. This entry follows that. It does not call the person "a third-batch transmitter", which would treat two documents as one, and it does not add 19 and 9 together.

## The transmitted project

Both documents give the same project name, 溧水剪纸 (Lishui paper-cutting). The category appears only in Annex 2 of the 2021 document, as 传统美术; Annex 3 of the 2017 document gives no category. The subject matter, technique and lineage of the project, and what this person does within it, are recorded in neither document. The project itself has its own entry on this library's culture site (`ls:item:lishui-jianzhi`), which carries the project's record, and this entry points to it across sites.

## Relation to Lishui

What the register supplies is a column headed "unit or address", here 永阳街道通济街社区, transcribed as written with no conversion, no rewriting of the community name and no added present-day equivalent; the 2017 document gives no address at all. That column evidences transmitting activity in Lishui, not place of birth, so the `relation` value is 活动 (active in Lishui) rather than 本地出生.

## Points of doubt

First, Annex 3 states 19 persons in total, while this library verified only 14 name entries. The names it did not verify are neither written nor supplied here, and the stated total is not used to reconstruct the list.

Second, the two documents claim the same batch while attaching different lists — a 19-person transmitter list against a 9-person transmitter register, issued by different bodies. Which one is the formal register is not recorded in the sources; this entry does not decide, and does not merge or add up the counts.

Third, the birth month appears in one place only, the 2021 annex, written 1965年12月. It is transcribed as written and not converted into an age.

Fourth, sex, native place, place of birth, teaching lineage, life events, honours, current position and the present state of transmitting are recorded in neither document. This entry does not guess and does not fill the gaps with common sense.

Fifth, the body of the 2021 notice summarises the batch as six representative projects and nine representative transmitters, naming Rui Yongmei, Liu Wenju and Yang Baihai as examples without listing all nine. This entry rests only on row 1 of the annex table and adds nothing from that summary.

Sixth, whether the 2017 entry 李雪皎（溧水剪纸） and row 1 of the 2021 annex are the same person and the same transmitting relationship is stated by neither document. The two records are set side by side as same name and same project, without a cross-document identification.
