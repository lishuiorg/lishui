Zhang Wendao is one of the Qing filial sons of Lishui listed in juan 7 of the Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, in its "Filial Sons" biography section. His biography is one of the shortest in the volume: one summary clause and one deed. This entry writes nothing beyond those two clauses and places everything the gazetteer does not record under Points of doubt rather than supplying it.

## The gazetteer text

> 張問道，幼年克孝。母病，割股和藥以進，尋愈。
> —— 康熙《溧水县志》卷之七·孝子传

## What the gazetteer records

"Truly filial in childhood" is the gazetteer's summary; "in childhood" is the period of the deed as the gazetteer states it, not a birth record, and this entry infers no birth year from it and converts nothing. His mother fell ill; he cut his thigh, compounded it into medicine and gave it to her, and she soon recovered. "Soon recovered" is the gazetteer's statement on the outcome. What the illness was, how the cutting and the treatment were carried out, and in what year she recovered are nowhere recorded. The biography gives no township, no native place, no origin and says nothing about any award.

## To be kept apart from the entry on Zhang Wenxing

This library has a separate entry for Zhang Wenxing (`ls:person:zhang-wenxing`), whose name differs from this one by a single character and whose record comes from a different volume and section of the gazetteer. This library has no material to decide whether the two are the same man, relatives, or wholly unconnected; the two therefore remain independent entries, are not merged, and are not cross-identified, and this entry does not list the other in `related`. Near-name checking is recorded here only as doubt, not as any identification.

## Relation to Lishui

The gazetteer gives Zhang Wendao no township and no native place; it ties him to this county only by placing him in the filial sons section. This entry enters him as "active in Lishui": what the text proves is that he is recorded in this county's biography section, not that he was born here, so it does not record him as "born locally". Apart from the cutting of his thigh for his mother, the biography touches no Lishui affair.

## Points of doubt

First, his birth and death years are not recorded; township and native place are unrecorded; the biography carries no dates, and this entry converts and infers nothing.

Second, "in childhood" is the period of the deed as the gazetteer states it and cannot yield a birth year.

Third, his origin is not recorded, this entry supplies no examination title, and his mother's name, birth and death are unrecorded.

Fourth, the nature of the illness, the course of the cutting and treatment, and the year of recovery are all unrecorded.

Fifth, the biography does not say whether he received an award, and this entry infers none from other entries in this volume.

Sixth, the entry for Zhang Wenxing differs by one character; this library has no material relating the two, and they remain independent, unmerged and not cross-identified.

Seventh, the era field "Qing" here follows the dynasty grouping of the "present dynasty" block of this biography section, not a birth record.

Eighth, this entry rests on a single biography in juan 7; the volume used here is an online transcription, not checked against a punctuated edition or the printed text, so citations must be marked as from the transcription.
