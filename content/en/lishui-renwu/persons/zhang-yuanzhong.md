Zhang Yuanzhong is a representative bearer this library found only in the 2017 document, and the annex puts him on the same row as another bearer. This entry records only what that row says and supplies no life story for either person.

## List status

The notice of the Lishui District People's Government publishing the third batch of district-level protected sites, intangible cultural heritage projects and representative transmitters (Li Zheng Fa [2017] No. 197) has an annex 3 headed "list of representative transmitters (19 in total)". This library read 14 names in it; Zhang Yuanzhong appears in one of the rows read, which places two names under one item name:

> 张远忠、蒋婷（中医）

That annex gives only the names and the item name, not a birth month or an address.

The notice of the General Office of the Lishui District People's Government (Li Zheng Ban Fa [2021] No. 3) has an annex 2 headed "third batch of district-level intangible cultural heritage representative bearer roster (9 in total)". This library read all nine rows, and Zhang Yuanzhong is not among them; this library does not reach him through that document.

## The item he is listed under

The item name Chinese medicine (中医) appears both among the item names of annex 2 of the 2017 document — the district intangible cultural heritage list of 22 items — and in the row naming him. Annex 2 of the same document carries a further item name, rendered in this library's glossary as Yang Family Medicine of Luoshan. Whether the two are one and the same item was not reached from the annexes, and this entry does not merge them. This library has not reached a formal full name for the Chinese medicine item, so it writes only the short name as given and does not expand it. Annex 3 gives no item category.

## Relation to Lishui

Annex 3 of the 2017 document gives no "unit or address", only the names and the item name. The roster is a district-level roster of Lishui, published by the Lishui District People's Government, and what a bearer roster evidences is transmitting activity in Lishui, not place of birth, so this entry's `relation` is "activity".

## Points of doubt

First, annex 3 places Zhang Yuanzhong and Jiang Ting on one row under the single item name Chinese medicine. What each of the two transmits, and whether they come from one line of transmission, is not recorded, and this entry does not guess.

Second, Chinese medicine is the short name as written in the annexes; its formal full name was not reached, and whether it is the same item as the one rendered as Yang Family Medicine of Luoshan was likewise not reached. Whether any municipal or provincial list carries a corresponding item falls outside the scope of these two documents.

Third, both documents call themselves the third batch while their rosters differ. All nine bearers of the 2021 annex 2 were read and Zhang Yuanzhong is not among them; annex 3 of the 2017 document claims 19 bearers, but this library read only 14, and the names it did not read are neither written nor supplied. This library does not decide which roster is the official one.

Fourth, the 2017 annex 3 records no birth month and no unit or address, and this entry does not guess at either.

Fifth, his sex, native place, place of birth, teachers and lineage, life story and present situation are recorded in none of the sources this library holds; sex in particular must not be inferred from the characters of a name.
