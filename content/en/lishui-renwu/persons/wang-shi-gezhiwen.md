Madam Wang is one of the chaste widows of Lishui recorded in juan 7, the "Chaste and Martyred Women" biography, of the Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*. The gazetteer gives only her natal surname Wang, her husband the late garrison commander Ge Zhiwen, and three things: his title, the year of her widowhood, and her keeping of chastity while raising an orphan. The passage sits under a heading "Sojourners"; this library judges that word a suspect heading, as explained in its own section below. This entry rests on a single item in this gazetteer and adds nothing the text does not record.

## The gazetteer text

> 王氏，故守備葛之文妻。年廿四喪夫，守節存孤。
> —— 康熙《溧水县志》卷之七·贞烈传

## Reading

The item is very short.

"Madam Wang, wife of the late garrison commander Ge Zhiwen" gives her natal surname and her husband's title. "Late" means deceased; "garrison commander" is the military post the gazetteer records for him, kept as such, without changing it to another rank or recording where and when he served. His courtesy name, native place, and career are unrecorded, as are her given name, residence, and paternal family.

"Widowed at twenty-four, she kept her chastity and raised an orphan" gives her age at widowhood and its substance: her husband died when she was twenty-four; she kept her chastity and raised the orphan. "Twenty-four" is the gazetteer's age; keeping chastity and raising the orphan is its summary; the child's name, the year of her husband's death, and the span of her chastity are unrecorded.

The gazetteer gives no detail of her chastity, and this entry does not supply any on its behalf, recording the gaps only under points of doubt.

## The suspect heading "Sojourners"

The passage this item belongs to carries a heading "Sojourners" in the transcription. This library judges the word a suspect heading: what follows (Cheng Tianmin's daughter, the Chen daughter, Wu Ai, Madam Ma, and others) are still chaste widows and martyred women, and does not match the sense of "sojourners". This library keeps the word as recorded and marks it a suspect heading, and does not read this item as belonging to a sojourner biography; whether the passage continues the Chaste and Martyred Women biography or is another biography must be settled against the printed edition.

## Relation to Lishui

This entry enters her as "active in Lishui", and the reason must be stated. What the gazetteer records is her chastity in Lishui: wife of the late garrison commander Ge Zhiwen, widowed at twenty-four, keeping chastity and raising an orphan, her home being the Lishui area. But the gazetteer records no birthplace or native place for her, naming her only "Madam Wang", so this entry does not record her as "born locally". There is no office, recommendation, or writing to attach to her — her husband's command was a military post and is not used here for "official service" — so "official service", "recommended from Lishui", and "writings" do not apply; since the record is of keeping her home, not of lodging abroad, "sojourn" does not apply either. Of the five categories only "active in Lishui" holds, and this entry uses it.

## Points of doubt

First, her birth and death years are unrecorded. The gazetteer gives only that she was widowed at twenty-four; it gives no year of birth, no year for her husband, and no year of death, and this entry converts and infers nothing.

Second, her birthplace and native place are not explicitly recorded. The gazetteer calls her only "Madam Wang"; her paternal family and residence are unrecorded, and this entry does not infer a native place from her husband's title.

Third, beyond her husband's name, her paternal family and offspring are not fully recorded. The gazetteer gives only her husband Ge Zhiwen; his courtesy name and where and when he served are unrecorded, as are the orphan's name and later career.

Fourth, "garrison commander" is the gazetteer's title for her husband, kept as such, without filling in the beginning and end of his service from other volumes.

Fifth, the honours the gazetteer records may not be complete. This item does not say whether she received an award, and this entry infers none from other items.

Sixth, the item is very short, with almost no deeds beyond her husband's name, her age at widowhood, and her keeping of chastity, and this entry does not draw on other items to fill it out.

Seventh, this entry rests on a single item in this gazetteer, in the "Sojourners" (suspect heading) block of the Chaste and Martyred Women biography; the volume is an online transcription, not checked against a punctuated edition or the printed text, so citations must be marked as from the transcription.

Eighth, searches across the other source layers of this library produced nothing that could add to her.
