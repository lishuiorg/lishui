Yi Xiaoyi is one of the seven men in the "Song" block of the Filial Sons biography in juan 7 of the Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*. That biography records where a man lived, an act of filiality, and the year of a commendation; it records no birth or death years, no family, and no finer township affiliation for the men. This entry writes only what this line records and supplies no life for him.

## Where the gazetteer places him

> 伊小乙，城中人。
> — Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, juan 7

The gazetteer writes only "a man of the town", three characters. This is one of the residences the biography records: he lived in the town, and the gazetteer records neither which township he belonged to, nor where his house stood, nor where he was born. This entry records the residence and supplies no township and no address.

## What the gazetteer records him doing

> 剖腹取肝以療母疾。
> — Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, juan 7

Cutting open the belly and taking the liver is an act of filiality as the gazetteer records it; this entry reproduces the gazetteer's wording and marks its nature: it is the gazetteer's account, and this entry judges neither its medical plausibility nor offers a modern explanation, and does not present it as a verifiable fact. The gazetteer does not say what illness his mother had, whether the cure succeeded, or in what year it happened.

## The commendation the gazetteer records

> 乾道間聞於朝，旌其里曰「表孝」。
> — Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, juan 7

"In the Qiandao era" is the year of the commendation as the gazetteer gives it; this entry reproduces it without converting it to a Common-Era year and infers from it neither his birth and death nor the year of the act. "Reported to the court" and "his ward was commended" are the two clauses the gazetteer records: the matter was reported upward, and his ward received a commendatory name. "Model Filiality" is that name as the gazetteer words it, and this entry reproduces the two characters.

## The link with the line on Liu Xingzu

The same biography has another line calling Liu Xingzu "a resident of the Model Filiality quarter", and this library holds a separate entry for him; the name "Model Filiality" appears in both. This entry states the link and also states what cannot be decided: first, whether the name of that quarter came from this very commendation of Yi Xiaoyi's ward is nowhere recorded, and this library has no material to decide it; second, the Liu Xingzu line says only "resident of the Model Filiality quarter" and never says whether that quarter and Yi Xiaoyi's ward are the same place, and this library has no material to decide that either. The two lines are linked only by shared wording; both doubts are recorded side by side, undecided, and the two entries are not merged.

## Relation to Lishui

The gazetteer places him as "a man of the town", which is a residence, not a birth record. He enters the persons site with the relation "Active in Lishui", and the reasoning must be stated: the locality the biography gives is where a man lived, not where he was born — the biography records no birthplace for anyone, only residence and where the act took place, so "born in Lishui" is not available; the gazetteer records no Lishui office, no writing, and no recommendation out of Lishui into office elsewhere, so "official service", "writings" and "Recommended from Lishui" are not available; what it records is that he practised filiality in the town of Lishui and that his ward was commended for it, which is activity in Lishui, so this entry uses "Active in Lishui".

## Points of doubt

First, his birth and death years are nowhere recorded, his family is nowhere recorded, and neither his mother's illness nor the outcome of the cure is recorded.

Second, "in the Qiandao era" dates only the commendation and cannot be used as evidence of his birth, death, or the date of his activity; this entry converts nothing and infers no year for the act.

Third, "a man of the town" is the biography's word for his residence; which township he belonged to and where his house stood are unrecorded, and this library has no material to decide them.

Fourth, "cutting open the belly and taking the liver" is the gazetteer's account, and its nature is marked in the body of this entry; this entry judges no plausibility and presents it as no modern fact.

Fifth, the relation between "Model Filiality" here and the "Model Filiality quarter" of the Liu Xingzu line — whether the quarter's name came from this commendation, and whether the two refer to one place — cannot be decided from this library's material, and both doubts stand side by side undecided.

Sixth, the order of the seven Song lines does not follow the commendation years the block gives, since two of them record no year at all; the order therefore cannot be used as evidence of sequence, and this entry infers no dates from it.

Seventh, this entry rests on one line of the biography; searches across the other source layers of this library produced nothing that could add to him.
