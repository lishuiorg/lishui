Yang Baihai is a Luoshan Great Dragon bearer this library reaches only through the 2021 document. This entry records what that document says and supplies no life story.

## List status

The notice of the General Office of the Lishui District People's Government publishing the third batch of district-level representative intangible cultural heritage items and bearer rosters (Li Zheng Ban Fa [2021] No. 3) has an annex 2 headed "third batch of district-level intangible cultural heritage representative bearer roster (9 in total)". Its columns are "no. | bearer | year and month of birth | item name | item category | unit or address". This library read all nine rows, and Yang Baihai is row 6, transcribed as written:

> 6｜杨白海｜1966年6月｜骆山大龙｜传统舞蹈｜和凤镇骆山社区

The body of the same notice states how many items and how many bearers were assessed and names some of them, Yang Baihai among the names given:

> 评定麦芽糖制作技艺、竹编制作技艺、新农村虾子灯等 6 项区级非遗代表性保护项目，芮咏梅、刘文菊、杨白海等 9 名非遗代表性传承人

The year and month of birth and the unit or address are kept exactly as written: no age is computed from them, and the community name is not rewritten.

The notice of the Lishui District People's Government publishing the third batch of district-level protected sites, intangible cultural heritage projects and representative transmitters (Li Zheng Fa [2017] No. 197) has an annex 3 headed "list of representative transmitters (19 in total)". This library read 14 names in it, and Yang Baihai is not among the 14 read. Because that annex claims 19 while this library read only 14, this library cannot tell whether the five names it did not read include Yang Baihai; this entry therefore says only that the bearer is not reached through that document, not that the bearer is absent from the list.

## The item listed under

Row 6 gives the item name Luoshan Great Dragon and the item category traditional dance, both as written in the annex columns, and this entry writes only those two names. The history of the item, the shape of the dragon, the origin legend and its national-level status belong to the Luoshan Great Dragon entry on the culture site (`ls:item:luoshan-dalong`) and are not repeated here.

## Relation to Lishui

What annex 2 supplies is the "unit or address" column, written as Luoshan Community, Hefeng Town. The roster is a district-level roster of Lishui, published by the General Office of the Lishui District People's Government, and what a bearer roster evidences is transmitting activity in Lishui — here in Luoshan Community of Hefeng Town — not place of birth, so this entry's `relation` is "activity". The village where the dragon dance is transmitted has its own entry on the towns site (`ls:place:luoshan-village`).

## Points of doubt

First, both documents call themselves the third batch while their rosters differ: annex 2 of the 2021 document claims 9 bearers and this library read all of them, annex 3 of the 2017 document claims 19 and this library read only 14. This library does not decide which roster is the official one, writes what each document records separately, and does not merge the counts.

Second, Yang Baihai does not appear among the 14 names read in the 2017 annex, and since that annex claims 19, whether the five names this library did not read include the bearer cannot be judged; nothing is asserted either way.

Third, the name Yang Baihai is close in writing to Yang Muhai, who appears in this library's Luoshan Great Dragon entry as a bearer listed in the national register. Whether the two names denote one and the same person cannot be judged from the material this library holds, and this entry makes no finding on the question.

Fourth, the record goes no further than a year and month of birth, and no age is computed from it. Sex, native place, place of birth, teachers and lineage, life events and present situation are recorded in none of the sources this library holds; sex in particular must not be inferred from the characters of a name.

Fifth, the roster gives an item name and an item category only. What part the bearer takes in the dragon dance, how the transmission is carried out, and to whom it is transmitted are not recorded. The standing of the item itself in the national and district lists, its batch and its safeguarding unit belong to the item entry, not to this one.
