Yang Dingxing is a representative bearer this library found only in the 2017 document. The roster gives him a name and an item name and nothing else, and this entry does not go beyond that.

## List status

The notice of the Lishui District People's Government publishing the third batch of district-level protected sites, intangible cultural heritage projects and representative transmitters (Li Zheng Fa [2017] No. 197) has an annex 3 headed "list of representative transmitters (19 in total)". This library read 14 names in it; Yang Dingxing is one of them, written as "Yang Dingxing (clay sculpture)". That annex gives only the name and the item name, not a birth month or an address.

The notice of the General Office of the Lishui District People's Government (Li Zheng Ban Fa [2021] No. 3) has an annex 2 headed "third batch of district-level intangible cultural heritage representative bearer roster (9 in total)". This library read all nine rows, and Yang Dingxing is not among them; this library does not reach him through that document.

## The item he transmits

The item name clay sculpture (泥塑) appears both among the item names of annex 2 of the 2017 document — the district intangible cultural heritage list of 22 items — and in his row of annex 3. It is the name as written in the annexes. The caution section of this library's source card states that such names are the original short forms and that the formal full item names must be checked against the annexes themselves; this library has not reached a formal full name, so this entry writes only clay sculpture and does not expand it into a "making craft" of its own invention. Annex 3 gives no item category.

## Relation to Lishui

Annex 3 of the 2017 document gives no "unit or address", only the name and the item name. The roster is a district-level roster of Lishui, published by the Lishui District People's Government, and what a bearer roster evidences is transmitting activity in Lishui, not place of birth, so this entry's `relation` is "activity".

## Points of doubt

First, both documents call themselves the third batch while their rosters differ. All nine bearers of the 2021 annex 2 were read and Yang Dingxing is not among them; annex 3 of the 2017 document claims 19 bearers, but this library read only 14, and the names it did not read are neither written nor supplied. This library does not decide which roster is the official one.

Second, clay sculpture is the short name as written in the annexes; its formal full name was not reached, and whether any municipal or provincial list carries a corresponding item falls outside the scope of these two documents.

Third, the 2017 annex 3 records no birth month and no unit or address, and this entry does not guess at either.

Fourth, his sex, native place, place of birth, teachers and lineage, life story and present situation are recorded in none of the sources this library holds.
