Madam Cao is one of the chaste widows of Lishui recorded in juan 7, the "Chaste and Martyred Women" biography, of the Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*. The gazetteer gives only her natal surname Cao, her husband the county-school student Zhou Chugao, and three things: his student status, her keeping chastity from twenty-five, and an honour from a former magistrate. The passage sits under a heading "Sojourners"; this library judges that word a suspect heading, as explained in its own section below. This entry rests on a single item in this gazetteer and adds nothing the text does not record.

## The gazetteer text

> 曹氏，庠生周初暠妻。廿五歲守節，前縣令獎之。
> —— 康熙《溧水县志》卷之七·贞烈传

## Reading

The item is very short.

"Madam Cao, wife of the county-school student Zhou Chugao" gives her natal surname and her husband's status. "County-school student" is the rank the gazetteer records for him, a student of the local county school; the name is kept as written, without supplying a courtesy name from other books. Her given name, courtesy name, residence, and paternal family are all unrecorded.

"From twenty-five she kept her chastity, and a former magistrate honoured her" gives two things: her keeping chastity from the age of twenty-five, and an honour from a former magistrate. The gazetteer says only "former magistrate" without a name, kept as such, and this entry neither supplies a name nor infers from other items in this volume which magistrate it was. The form and text of the honour, and the year, are unrecorded.

## The suspect heading "Sojourners"

The passage this item belongs to carries a heading "Sojourners" in the transcription. This library judges the word a suspect heading: what follows (Cheng Tianmin's daughter, the Chen daughter, Wu Ai, Madam Ma, and others) are still chaste widows and martyred women, and does not match the sense of "sojourners". This library keeps the word as recorded and marks it a suspect heading, and does not read this item as belonging to a sojourner biography; whether the passage continues the Chaste and Martyred Women biography or is another biography must be settled against the printed edition.

## Relation to Lishui

This entry enters her as "active in Lishui", and the reason must be stated. What the gazetteer records is her chastity in Lishui: wife of the county-school student Zhou Chugao, keeping chastity from twenty-five and honoured by a former magistrate — her husband a student of the county school and the honouring official a Lishui magistrate. But the gazetteer records no birthplace or native place for her, naming her only "Madam Cao", so this entry does not record her as "born locally". There is no office, recommendation, or writing to attach to her, so "official service", "recommended from Lishui", and "writings" do not apply; since the record is of keeping her home, not of lodging abroad, "sojourn" does not apply either. Of the five categories only "active in Lishui" holds, and this entry uses it.

## Points of doubt

First, her birth and death years are unrecorded. The gazetteer gives only that she kept her chastity from twenty-five; it gives no year of birth, no year for her husband, and no year of death, and this entry converts and infers nothing.

Second, her birthplace and native place are not explicitly recorded. The gazetteer calls her only "Madam Cao"; her paternal family and residence are unrecorded.

Third, beyond her husband's name, her paternal family and offspring are not fully recorded. The gazetteer gives only her husband the student Zhou Chugao; his courtesy name and whether they had children are unrecorded.

Fourth, the "former magistrate" is unnamed, kept as such; this entry neither supplies a name nor infers which magistrate it was, and the form and text of the honour and its year are likewise unrecorded.

Fifth, the item is very short, with almost no deeds beyond her husband's status, her age at the start of her chastity, and the honour, and this entry does not draw on other items to fill it out.

Sixth, this entry rests on a single item in this gazetteer, in the "Sojourners" (suspect heading) block of the Chaste and Martyred Women biography; the volume is an online transcription, not checked against a punctuated edition or the printed text, so citations must be marked as from the transcription.

Seventh, searches across the other source layers of this library produced nothing that could add to her.
