Chen Sen is one of the men presented from Lishui and served elsewhere listed in the "Yuan" block of the Recommendations examination in juan 2 of the Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*. The section gives him two items — the route of presentation and the office held — and nothing else. This entry writes only those two items and supplies no life for him.

## Route into office and office held

> 陳森由人材，任縣尉。
> — Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, juan 2

"By way of persons of talent" is the category of presentation as the gazetteer words it; this entry reproduces it and does not convert it into "jinshi" or any other degree. "Served as a county constable" is the office as the gazetteer words it — a bare title that does not say which county he served in. This entry supplies no county name, and does not write "constable of Lishui county" merely because he is named in a Lishui gazetteer: many other lines of the same section state the place of service plainly (a prefecture of Zhejiang, a county of Fujian), while this line does not, so the place of his post is unrecorded. What a constable did, and when he took up and left the post, is nowhere recorded. The "Yuan" block gives no years at all, so neither the year of presentation nor the year of appointment can be known.

## Relation to Lishui

The gazetteer gives no native place and records no act of his in Lishui. He enters the persons site with the relation "Recommended from Lishui", and the reasoning must be stated: the only tie the section makes between him and Lishui is that he was presented from Lishui and served elsewhere — being named in the Recommendations examination of a Lishui gazetteer is itself the gazetteer's statement that he was recommended out of Lishui and held office away from it. This value is used only for this section, and differs from the others: the section records no birthplace, so "born in Lishui" is not available; the county of his post is not given and the section records no Lishui post of his, so "official service" is not available; the section records no activity of his in Lishui, so "active in Lishui" is not available.

## Points of doubt

First, his birth and death years, his native place and his courtesy name are nowhere recorded.

Second, the era value "Five Dynasties, Song and Yuan" here only records that the section places him in its "Yuan" block. That grouping dates his presentation and service, not his birth: it proves neither that he was born in a particular Yuan year nor that he was active in any particular Yuan year, and this block gives no years at all.

Third, his route into office is given only as "by way of persons of talent", which must not be rewritten as "jinshi" or any other degree; "county constable" is a bare office title, and which county he served in, what he did and when are unrecorded.

Fourth, whether he had a house, descendants or remains in Lishui is nowhere recorded, and this library has no material to decide it.

Fifth, this entry rests on one roster line in the "Yuan" block; searches across the other source layers of this library produced nothing that could add to him.
