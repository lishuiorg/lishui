Li Ganbin has one record in this library, in Annex 3 of the 2017 notice of the People's Government of Lishui District. This entry states what that record says and why the other document does not contain him.

## Registry status

**Li Zheng Fa [2017] No. 197 (2017).** The notice of the People's Government of Lishui District on publishing the third batch of district-level protected sites, district-level intangible cultural heritage projects and district-level transmitters is dated 22 November 2017 and was posted on the district government website in October 2018; the batch year follows the year carried by the document number. Its Annex 3 is headed "List of representative transmitters (19 persons in total)". This library verified 14 name entries in it. One row is written 李赣宾、李红燕（剪纸） — two names on a single row sharing one project name, 剪纸. This entry transcribes the row as written, without splitting it or rewriting it. The annex gives name and project only, with no birth date, no project category and no address.

**Li Zheng Ban Fa [2021] No. 3 (2021).** The notice of the General Office of the People's Government of Lishui District on publishing the third batch of district-level representative projects and representative transmitters is dated 11 January 2021. Its Annex 2 is headed "Third batch of district-level intangible cultural heritage representative transmitters (9 persons in total)", and this library transcribed all nine rows of that table; none of them carries the name Li Ganbin. This entry does not build a second record from that document, and it does not treat the absence as a denial of the record in Annex 3.

Both documents label themselves the third batch while attaching lists of different scope. This entry reports separately what each one records, does not merge them, does not add their counts, and does not decide which list is the formal one.

## The transmitted project

The project name in the 2017 document is written 剪纸, not 溧水剪纸. The same annex separately records 李雪皎（溧水剪纸）, so the two name forms differ within one annex, and this entry quotes each document as written instead of harmonising them. Neither document gives a project category for this person — the 2021 annex has a category column but does not reach him. The source card notes that the names in that annex are given as written and that the full formal project names still have to be checked against the annex itself. This library's entry for the paper-cutting project is the one titled 溧水剪纸 on the culture site (`ls:item:lishui-jianzhi`); this entry points to it across sites but does not use it to settle this person's relation to the project.

## Relation to Lishui

Annex 3 of the 2017 document has no "unit or address" column, and Annex 2 of the 2021 document has one but does not reach this person, so this library holds no address for him in Lishui. The one thing the records do locate is that a Lishui District register of district-level intangible cultural heritage transmitters lists him, which evidences transmitting activity in Lishui and not place of birth. The `relation` value is therefore 活动 (active in Lishui) rather than 本地出生.

## Points of doubt

First, Annex 3 states 19 persons in total, while this library verified only 14 name entries. The names it did not verify are neither written nor supplied here, and the stated total is not used to reconstruct the list.

Second, the two documents claim the same batch while attaching different lists — a 19-person transmitter list against a 9-person transmitter register, issued by different bodies. Which one is the formal register is not recorded in the sources; this entry does not decide, and does not merge or add up the counts.

Third, neither document gives a birth month or an address for this person. This entry does not convert anything into an age and does not supply an address.

Fourth, sex, native place, place of birth, teaching lineage, life events, honours and the present state of transmitting are recorded in neither document. This entry does not guess and does not fill the gaps with common sense.

Fifth, the original row writes Li Ganbin and Li Hongyan together under one project name. Whether the two are of the same school, the same family or the same place is not recorded, and this entry does not guess.

Sixth, whether the name form 剪纸 and the name form 溧水剪纸 denote one and the same project, and what the full formal project name is, could not be verified against the annex text.
