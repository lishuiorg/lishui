Gao Chong is one of the Ming officials of Lishui listed in juan 6 of the Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*. His biography is very short: two clauses of origin, one of examination and appointment, two of judgement. This entry records it as it stands, and states what this volume's year of appointment can and cannot be used for.

## Origin and term of appointment

> 高翀，江西新淦人，湖广安陆籍。由进士，嘉靖七年任
> — Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, juan 6

He was a native of Xin'gan in Jiangxi, with a registered origin in Anlu in Huguang, a *jinshi* holder, and was appointed in the seventh year of the Jiajing era. The gazetteer gives origin twice — once as a native of Xin'gan, once with registration in Anlu — and this entry keeps both as they stand, deciding neither which is the native line nor which the registered one, and deleting neither. "The seventh year of the Jiajing era" is a definite year in this volume covering only his arrival; nothing is converted into a Common Era year. The biography never uses the word "Lishui"; his service there follows from the volume itself, which lists the officials of Lishui. The gazetteer also never gives his title in Lishui, and this entry does not assume that of county magistrate.

## What the gazetteer records

> 廉明而威，民无咎者。
> — Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, juan 6

Both clauses are judgement: clean, clear and with authority. The second clause, as transcribed, can be read two ways — that the people incurred no punishment, or that they had no one to blame — and the gazetteer does not resolve it, so this entry quotes the wording as it stands without forcing a reading. The biography records no building, no fiscal measure, no case and no school measure in Lishui, and none is inferred from the judgement.

## Relation to Lishui

He was not a Lishui native and enters this library through official service. What ties him to the county is the statement of his appointment and the two clauses of judgement.

One relation to this library's entry on Wang Congshan must be stated. This volume separately records Gao Chong as appointed in the seventh year of the Jiajing era and Zhang Wenxing in the ninth, and the entry on Wang Congshan uses those two to set out three incompatible accounts of the lower limit of his term in Lishui. This entry records only what this volume says about Gao Chong's appointment: the volume never gives the year his predecessor handed over, so this entry does not use "appointed in the seventh year" to conclude that Wang Congshan had left office by then. A successor's arrival shows only that someone took up the post that year; it records nothing about when the previous holder left.

## Points of doubt

First, his birth and death years are not recorded; origin appears twice, once as a native of Xin'gan in Jiangxi and once with registration in Anlu in Huguang, and the gazetteer does not say which is the native line.

Second, precision of the term: "the seventh year of the Jiajing era" is a definite year in this volume and covers only his arrival; the year he left Lishui is not recorded, and nothing is converted into a Common Era year.

Third, his title in Lishui is not given, and this entry does not write it as county magistrate.

Fourth, the second clause of judgement admits two readings the gazetteer does not resolve, and this entry quotes it without forcing a reading.

Fifth, this volume records no year of handover by his predecessor, and this entry does not use his appointment year to fix any predecessor's year of departure.

Sixth, any concrete act in Lishui, his later career, his final rank and his place of death are all unrecorded.

Seventh, this entry rests on one biography in this volume, an online transcription not checked against a punctuated or printed edition; searches across the other source layers of this library produced nothing that could add to him.
