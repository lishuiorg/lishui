Madam Yuan is one of the chaste widows of Lishui recorded in juan 7, the "Chaste and Martyred Women" biography, of the Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*. The gazetteer gives only her natal surname Yuan, her husband Zhou Tianqi, and two things: over fifty years of chastity, and an honour from the magistrate An Yingsui. The passage sits under a heading "Sojourners"; this library judges that word a suspect heading, as explained in its own section below. This entry rests on a single item in this gazetteer and adds nothing the text does not record.

## The gazetteer text

> 袁氏，周天奇妻。守節五十餘年，知縣安應睟獎之。
> —— 康熙《溧水县志》卷之七·贞烈传

## Reading

The item is very short, only two sentences.

"Madam Yuan, wife of Zhou Tianqi" gives her natal surname and her husband's name. Her given name, courtesy name, residence, and paternal family are all unrecorded, as are her husband's courtesy name and status.

"She kept her chastity over fifty years, and the magistrate An Yingsui honoured her" gives the two matters. "Over fifty years" is the gazetteer's figure; the start and end of that span, the year of her marriage, and the year of her husband's death are all unrecorded. And "the magistrate An Yingsui honoured her" gives the honouring official as An Yingsui, magistrate of this county. The form of the honour, whether a plaque or a petition for official recognition, and its text are unrecorded, and this entry supplies nothing.

The gazetteer gives not a single detail of her chastity, and this entry does not supply on its behalf such things as her age, her deeds, or her offspring, recording the gaps only under points of doubt.

## The suspect heading "Sojourners"

The passage this item belongs to carries a heading "Sojourners" in the transcription. This library judges the word a suspect heading: what follows (Cheng Tianmin's daughter, the Chen daughter, Wu Ai, Madam Ma, and others) are still chaste widows and martyred women, and does not match the sense of "sojourners". This library keeps the word as recorded and marks it a suspect heading, and does not read this item as belonging to a sojourner biography; whether the passage continues the Chaste and Martyred Women biography or is another biography must be settled against the printed edition.

## Relation to Lishui

This entry enters her as "active in Lishui", and the reason must be stated. What the gazetteer records is her chastity in Lishui: wife of Zhou Tianqi, keeping her chastity over fifty years and finally honoured by the county magistrate An Yingsui, the honouring official a Lishui officer. But the gazetteer records no birthplace or native place for her, naming her only "Madam Yuan", so this entry does not record her as "born locally". There is no office, recommendation, or writing to attach to her, so "official service", "recommended from Lishui", and "writings" do not apply; since the record is of keeping her home, not of lodging abroad, "sojourn" does not apply either. Of the five categories only "active in Lishui" holds, and this entry uses it.

## Points of doubt

First, her birth and death years are unrecorded. The gazetteer gives only over fifty years of chastity; it gives no year of birth, no year for her husband, and no year of death, and this entry converts and infers nothing.

Second, her birthplace and native place are not explicitly recorded. The gazetteer calls her only "Madam Yuan"; her paternal family and residence are unrecorded.

Third, beyond her husband's name, her paternal family and offspring are not fully recorded. The gazetteer gives only her husband Zhou Tianqi; his courtesy name, status, and whether they had children are all unrecorded.

Fourth, only the honouring official is recorded, not its form or text. The gazetteer says only that the magistrate An Yingsui honoured her; the year and the plaque text are unrecorded.

Fifth, the item is very short, with almost no deeds beyond her husband's name and the years of chastity, and this entry does not draw on other items in this volume to fill it out.

Sixth, this entry rests on a single item in this gazetteer, in the "Sojourners" (suspect heading) block of the Chaste and Martyred Women biography; the volume is an online transcription, not checked against a punctuated edition or the printed text, so citations must be marked as from the transcription.

Seventh, searches across the other source layers of this library produced nothing that could add to her.
