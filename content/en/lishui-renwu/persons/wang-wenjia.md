Wang Wenjia is a representative bearer this library found in only one of the two district-level documents that call themselves the "third batch". This entry records what that one document says; what the other document reached, and what it did not, is handled under "Points of doubt".

## List status

The notice of the General Office of the Lishui District People's Government publishing the third batch of district-level representative intangible cultural heritage items and bearers (Li Zheng Ban Fa [2021] No. 3) has an annex 2 headed "third batch of district-level intangible cultural heritage representative bearer roster (9 in total)". This library read all nine rows; Wang Wenjia is row 4, as written:

> 王文家｜1964年11月｜溧水竹编技艺｜传统技艺｜洪蓝街道蒲塘社区

Annex 1 of the same document, headed "third batch of district-level intangible cultural heritage representative item roster", lists six items; its row 5 gives the item name in the form rendered in this library's glossary as Bamboo Weaving, with the protecting unit given as the Jingqiao Town cultural and sports centre.

## The item he transmits

Annex 2 gives his item name in a form that reads "Lishui bamboo weaving", with the item category "traditional craft"; both are transcribed as written and are not expanded into another name. Within the same document the two annexes use different wording for the bamboo item — one "bamboo weaving", the other "Lishui bamboo weaving" — and this entry records both without merging them or deciding which is the formal name. This library holds a separate entry for the bamboo weaving item.

## Relation to Lishui

Annex 2 gives his "unit or address" as Putang Community in Honglan Subdistrict, quoted as written, with the community name unchanged. A bearer roster evidences transmitting activity in Lishui, not place of birth, so this entry's `relation` is "activity".

## Points of doubt

First, this library reaches him only through the 2021 document. Annex 3 of the 2017 notice (Li Zheng Fa [2017] No. 197) claims a "list of representative transmitters (19 in total)", but this library read only 14 names in it, and Wang Wenjia is not among those 14. Because five names in that annex were not read, this does not establish that he is absent from the 2017 list. Both documents call themselves the third batch while their rosters differ, and this library does not decide which roster is the official one.

Second, the two annexes of the same document differ in wording, and annex 1 places the protecting unit of the bamboo item in Jingqiao Town while annex 2 places Wang Wenjia in a community of Honglan Subdistrict. Whether the two refer to one and the same item, and whether he is the bearer of that item, was not reached by this library and is not asserted here.

Third, "Lishui bamboo weaving" is the name as written in the annex; the formal full name of the item has to be checked against the annex itself and was not reached by this library.

Fourth, the birth month "November 1964" is transcribed as written and is not converted into an age.

Fifth, his sex, native place, place of birth, teachers and lineage, life story and present situation are recorded in none of the sources this library holds.
