Yang Yuanli is one of the men presented from Lishui and served elsewhere listed in the "Ming, Hongwu period" block of the Recommendations examination in juan 2 of the Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*. The section gives him the route of presentation, the office held, and one clause about his retirement, and nothing else. This entry writes only those three items and supplies no life for him.

## Route into office and office held

> 楊原禮由人材，授戶部寶鈔提舉使。永樂二年有敕，致仕。
> — Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, juan 2

"By way of persons of talent" is the category of presentation as the gazetteer words it; this entry reproduces it and does not convert it into "jinshi" or any other degree. "Appointed superintendent of paper money under the Ministry of Revenue" is the office as the gazetteer words it — a bare title with no year of taking it up, and it lay away from Lishui. What a superintendent did, and how the office related to the paper money the gazetteer names, is nowhere explained, and this entry supplies nothing. The clause "in the second year of the Yongle era there was an edict, and he retired" is the only dated statement in this whole Hongwu block, and this entry reproduces it: the era year is the gazetteer's own and is converted to no Common-Era year; the content of the edict is not given at all; the gazetteer only sets the edict and the retirement side by side without stating a causal link, so this entry decides no cause and does not decide whether the retirement fell in that year.

## Relation to Lishui

The gazetteer gives no native place and records no act of his in Lishui. He enters the persons site with the relation "Recommended from Lishui", and the reasoning must be stated: the only tie the section makes between him and Lishui is that he was presented from Lishui and served elsewhere — being named in the Recommendations examination of a Lishui gazetteer is itself the gazetteer's statement that he was recommended out of Lishui and held office away from it. This value is used only for this section, and differs from the others: the section records no birthplace, so "born in Lishui" is not available; his office lay with the Ministry of Revenue and he held no Lishui post, so "official service" is not available; the section records no activity of his in Lishui, so "active in Lishui" is not available.

## Points of doubt

First, his birth and death years, his native place and his courtesy name are nowhere recorded; nor is anything after his retirement.

Second, the era value "Ming" here only records that the section places him in the Ming grouping of this examination. That grouping dates his presentation and service, not his birth: it proves neither that he was born in the Hongwu era nor that he was active in any particular Hongwu year.

Third, the grouping and the dating do not agree: the section lists him in its "Ming, Hongwu period" block while the only date in the line belongs to the Yongle era. This entry records both as they stand, changes no date to fit the grouping and no grouping to fit the date, and does not decide whether his presentation fell in the Hongwu or the Yongle era.

Fourth, his route into office is given only as "by way of persons of talent", which must not be rewritten as "jinshi" or any other degree; "superintendent of paper money under the Ministry of Revenue" is an office title whose duties and term are unrecorded.

Fifth, the content of the edict and its relation to the retirement are unrecorded, and nothing is decided about them.

Sixth, whether he had a house, descendants or remains in Lishui is nowhere recorded, and this library has no material to decide it.

Seventh, this entry rests on one roster line in the Hongwu block; searches across the other source layers of this library produced nothing that could add to him.
