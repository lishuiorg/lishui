Pan Zhen is an entry in the Song part of the notable-officials biography in juan 6 of the Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*. His biography is two sentences long, and this entry goes no further than they do.

## Origin and term

> 潘振，建炎间任为尉。
> —— 康熙《溧水县志》卷之六·名宦传

His appointment is dated only as "during the Jianyan era" — a span rather than a fixed year, quoted as it stands; this entry neither converts it into a specific year nor infers one from the dynasty. His Lishui office is stated plainly as "wei", and this entry follows the source and writes it as wei, not as "magistrate of the county": that a man holding the office of wei is placed among the notable officials shows that this volume's category is not limited to county magistrates. No native place is recorded.

## What the gazetteer records

> 会金人攻建康，陷溧水，振独死之。
> —— 康熙《溧水县志》卷之六·名宦传

The single act recorded is this: when the Jin attacked Jiankang and Lishui fell, "Zhen alone died for it". "Alone" is the gazetteer's own word and is reproduced here. The course of events, its date, and anything done after his death are recorded nowhere in the biography.

## Relation to Lishui

He enters the volume of notable officials because he held the office of wei in Lishui and died there when the county fell, and this entry records him through official service. His connection with Lishui is exactly that: he served there and died there.

## Points of doubt

First, his birth and death years are not recorded.

Second, his native place is not recorded.

Third, "during the Jianyan era" is a span: the gazetteer gives no fixed year, and this entry supplies no year of the common era and infers none from the dynasty.

Fourth, the gazetteer says only "wei" and gives no fuller title or office affiliation.

Fifth, the course and date of the fall of the county and of his death, and any posthumous honour, are recorded nowhere.

Sixth, this volume was read from an online transcription, not from a punctuated edition or the printed book, so every quotation is marked "from the online transcription".
