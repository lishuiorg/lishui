Tang Dayou is one of the figures in the Yuan section of the Righteous Men biography in juan 7 of the Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*. The notice records that his household lived five generations together, with nearly a hundred descendants and none selfish, and that his gate was honoured in the Dade period. This entry follows the gazetteer's layers, takes the role "local worthy" because he belongs to the Righteous Men biography, and states its limits.

## The gazetteer text

> 湯大有，五世同居，子孫殆百口，無一自私者。大德間表其門。
> — Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, juan 7, Righteous Men biography

## His name and station

"Tang Dayou" is the name the gazetteer gives, written here in its simplified form and with the slug tang-dayou. The gazetteer records no courtesy name or style name, no home place and no birth and death years, and none is supplied.

## Five generations living together

"Five generations lived together, with nearly a hundred descendants and not one selfish among them" is the gazetteer's account: five generations lived together in one household, with close to a hundred descendants, and not one of them self-seeking. "Five generations living together" and "nearly a hundred" are the gazetteer's own phrases and are reproduced; "nearly a hundred" is the gazetteer's approximate phrase, and this entry does not compute the household's exact number or supply its lineage and family rules. "Not one selfish among them" is the gazetteer's appraisal, and nothing is recorded as its basis.

## The honouring of his gate in the Dade period

"In the Dade period his gate was honoured" is the gazetteer's account of the honour he received: in the Dade period his gate was honoured, in recognition of his righteous household. The era is the gazetteer's own and is reproduced without conversion into a Common-Era year; in what year the honouring was done, on whose memorial, and under what title are unrecorded, and nothing is inferred here from other juan.

## Relation to Lishui

Of Tang Dayou the gazetteer records his five generations living together, nearly a hundred souls with none selfish, and the honouring of his gate. He enters this site with the relation "active in Lishui", and the reasoning must be stated: the gazetteer records no birth place, native place or township, so "born in Lishui" does not apply; it records no office he held in Lishui, no writings of his and no recommendation of him out of Lishui, so "official service", "writings" and "recommended from Lishui" do not apply; and the biography belongs to the "Righteous Men" section of this county's gazetteer and records his righteous household and the honour it received, which this library treats as his activity in Lishui, so the relation taken is "active in Lishui". His role is taken as "local worthy" because he was honoured as a righteous household and counts as one of this county's worthies, not on any ground for "official" or "teacher or scholar".

## Points of doubt

First, his birth and death years are not recorded; "the Dade period" dates only the honouring of his gate, and nothing is converted into a Common-Era year or used to infer his age.

Second, neither his courtesy name nor his style name is recorded.

Third, his birth place and native place are not explicitly recorded: the gazetteer gives no home place, so no native place can be decided, nor may he be called a native of Lishui merely for appearing in this county's Righteous Men biography.

Fourth, his lineage, family rules and descendants' names are unrecorded; "nearly a hundred" is an approximate phrase, and this entry does not compute the number.

Fifth, the number of years the five generations lived together and the beginning and end of the shared household are unrecorded.

Sixth, the year, memorial and title of the honouring of his gate are unrecorded, and nothing is inferred here from other juan.
