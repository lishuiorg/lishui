Madam Wu is one of the Ming-era chaste women of Lishui in the "Biographies of the Chaste" of juan 7 in the Kangxi Lishui County Gazetteer, recorded as the wife of Zhang Shixian. This entry rests on that single notice.

## Gazetteer text

> 吳氏，章時獻妻。年二十餘夫死，矢志撫孤，教二子為邑庠生。守節五十四年，直指屢旌其廬。
> —— 康熙《溧水县志》卷之七·贞烈传

## Reading of the text

First, "wife of Zhang Shixian" names husband and wife.

Second, "her husband died when she was in her twenties, and she resolved to raise the orphan." "In her twenties" is approximate.

Third, "she taught two sons to become county-school students." The gazetteer does not name the two sons.

Fourth, "she kept her chastity fifty-four years, and the circuit censor repeatedly honoured her gate." The number and dates of the honours are not given.

## Relation to Lishui

The gazetteer identifies her only through her husband, Zhang Shixian, whose household was her place of residence; her chastity, her teaching of her sons and the honours she received attach to the Lishui countryside. The text neither states her birthplace nor calls her a native of Lishui, so this entry does not mark her as "native of Lishui." She held no office, received no recommendation and left no writings, so the association is recorded as "active in Lishui," based on her conduct recorded there.

## Points of doubt

First, no birth or death years are given; "in her twenties" is approximate, and "fifty-four years" is the gazetteer's figure for her widowhood.

Second, her birthplace and native place are not stated; the gazetteer ties her only to her husband's name.

Third, the life, rank and death year of Zhang Shixian are unrecorded.

Fourth, the two sons are unnamed, and the year of their entering school and their later lives are not recorded.

Fifth, the repeated honours by the circuit censor give no number, year or tablet inscription.

Sixth, this entry rests on one notice in juan 7 of the Kangxi gazetteer, a network transcription not checked against a critical or printed edition.
