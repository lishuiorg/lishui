Huang Cheng is one of the Ming filial sons of Lishui listed in juan 7 of the Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, in its "Filial Sons" biography section. His biography has the same shape as the entry on Yu Qin and is one of the shortest in the volume: one clause of residence, one of cutting his thigh, one of commendation. This entry writes nothing beyond those three clauses.

## The gazetteer text

> 黃棖，居南隅。割股療父病，知縣傅應禎旌獎之。
> —— 康熙《溧水县志》卷之七·孝子传

## The name as written

The gazetteer writes his name with the character 棖. This entry uses the simplified form in its title and reproduces 棖 unchanged in every quotation; this library neither alters the gazetteer's character nor decides whether the two forms are to be distinguished.

## "Residing in the southern quarter"

The gazetteer gives his residence only as "the southern quarter". This is a directional designation, not a township name; what it actually covers — which township, which ward, what place today — is beyond this library's material, and this entry does not fix it on any village or hamlet. Whether it overlaps or can be matched with township names other entries in this volume record cannot be determined from this library's material. The entry on Yu Qin records "the northern quarter" and this one "the southern quarter"; both are directional only, and this entry infers neither that the two men lived together nor that their quarters adjoined.

## The cutting and the commendation

"Cut his thigh to treat his father's illness" is the biography's only deed — here for a father, where the entry on Yu Qin records it for a mother, and this entry keeps each as the gazetteer has it. "The district magistrate Fu Yingzhen commended him" gives both the commender and his title.

This must be read against an existing entry of this library. Fu Yingzhen appears three times in this volume: here and in the entry on Yu Qin as "the district magistrate Fu Yingzhen", and in the entry on Fu Pou as "Magistrate Fu Yingzhen" — it is this volume's Filial Sons section that gives Fu Yingzhen's Lishui title as district magistrate. This library already has an entry for Liu Yinglei (`ls:person:liu-yinglei`), whose biography in juan 6 contains the clause "the matter is set out in the record by Censor Fu Yingzhen", and the man referred to is this Fu Yingzhen; but that passage writes "Censor Fu", while this volume writes "district magistrate" and "Magistrate Fu": the two titles differ, one censor and one district magistrate. This library keeps both wordings side by side, decides neither that they are the same title nor that the man named in juan 6 must be the man named here, and the `related` link to the Liu Yinglei entry is a cross-reference for checking only. This library has no person entry for Fu Yingzhen, and this entry supplies no years for his Lishui term. The year of the commendation and its form are nowhere recorded.

## Relation to Lishui

The gazetteer records only that he resided in a quarter of the county, and this entry enters him as "active in Lishui". A residence is the place the gazetteer records, not a birth record: it gives no native place and no birthplace, so this entry does not record him as "born locally". What ties him to Lishui is his residence in a quarter of this county, the cutting of his thigh for his father, and his commendation by a magistrate of this county — the last being the only place where the biography ties him directly to a Lishui officeholder.

## Points of doubt

First, his birth and death years are not recorded; neither a township name nor a native place is recorded.

Second, "southern quarter" is directional only; this library has no material identifying it, and this entry neither fixes it on a village nor forces a match with the townships named elsewhere in this volume.

Third, his origin is not recorded, and this entry supplies no examination title.

Fourth, the nature of his father's illness, whether he recovered, and the year of the deed are all unrecorded.

Fifth, the year and the form of the commendation are unrecorded; this entry converts and infers nothing.

Sixth, "district magistrate Fu Yingzhen" here and "Censor Fu Yingzhen" in the officials' biography give two different titles; this library keeps both and decides nothing, and the `related` link is a cross-reference only, not an identification.

Seventh, this library does not decide the relationship between the character 棖 in the gazetteer and the simplified form used in this entry's title; it only reproduces the original.

Eighth, the era field "Ming" here follows the dynasty grouping of the "Ming" block of this biography section, not a birth record.

Ninth, this entry rests on a single biography in juan 7; the volume used here is an online transcription, not checked against a punctuated edition or the printed text, so citations must be marked as from the transcription. Searches across the other source layers of this library produced nothing that could add to him.
