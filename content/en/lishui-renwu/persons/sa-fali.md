Sa Fali is one of the figures in the Yuan section of the Chaste and Staunch biography in juan 7 of the Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*. The notice records her widowhood, her spinning and weaving to teach her son, the honouring of her gate, and her enfeoffment — in this section, an entry that runs through a whole life of chastity and the honours that followed it. This entry keeps those layers apart.

## The gazetteer text

> 薩法禮，寡而甚貧，勤織紡訓子。至治二年旌其門曰「貞節」，至順間以子貴，封容國夫人。
> — Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, juan 7, Chaste and Staunch biography

## Her name and station

"Sa Fali" is the designation the gazetteer gives, and this entry reproduces it, the slug taking its pinyin form sa-fali. The name appears not to be a Han surname and given name, so this library reproduces the gazetteer's characters without deciding her descent and without rewriting it as a Chinese name. "Widowed and very poor" is the gazetteer's account of her station: she was widowed and extremely poor. Her husband's name and the year of his death are unrecorded, as are the house she married into and the township she lived in.

## Spinning and weaving to teach her son

"That she span and wove diligently to teach her son" is the gazetteer's account of her conduct: she worked hard at spinning and weaving and taught her son by it. The words are the gazetteer's own and are reproduced; what she taught and who her son was are not recorded.

## The honouring of her gate and the enfeoffment

"In the second year of the Zhizhi era her gate was honoured as 'Chaste and Faithful'" is the gazetteer's account of the honour she received: in the second year of the Zhizhi era her gate was given the name "Chaste and Faithful". The era and year are the gazetteer's own and are reproduced without conversion into a Common-Era year. "In the Zhishun period, through her son's eminence, she was enfeoffed as Lady of Rong" is the gazetteer's account of the honour that followed: in the Zhishun period, because her son rose to eminence, she was enfeoffed as Lady of Rong. The era is reproduced without conversion, and the title is reproduced as the gazetteer gives it. Her son's name, office, and the reason for his rise are all unrecorded.

## Relation to Lishui

Of Sa Fali the gazetteer records her widowhood, her spinning and weaving to teach her son, the honouring of her gate and her enfeoffment. She enters this site with the relation "active in Lishui", and the reasoning must be stated: the gazetteer records no birth place, native place or township, so "born in Lishui" does not apply; it records no office she held in Lishui, no writings of hers and no recommendation of her out of Lishui, so "official service", "writings" and "recommended from Lishui" do not apply; and the biography belongs to the "Chaste and Staunch" section of this county's gazetteer and records her chastity together with the honours she received, which this library treats as her activity in Lishui, so the relation taken is "active in Lishui". The limits must be stated: the gazetteer records no home place for her, so "active in Lishui" here rests only on her appearing in this county's Chaste and Staunch biography and carries no explicit statement that she acted in Lishui.

## Points of doubt

First, her birth and death years are not recorded; "the second year of the Zhizhi era" and "the Zhishun period" date only the honouring and enfeoffment, and nothing is converted into a Common-Era year or used to infer her birth, death or age.

Second, her birth place and native place are not explicitly recorded: the gazetteer gives no home place, so no native place can be decided, nor may she be called a native of Lishui merely for appearing in this county's Chaste and Staunch biography.

Third, neither her husband's name nor his family is recorded.

Fourth, her son's name, office and the reason for his rise are unrecorded; "through her son's eminence" is the gazetteer's phrase, and nothing is supplied about her son's career from it.

Fifth, "Lady of Rong" is the title the gazetteer gives and is reproduced without any judgement as to the system or rank of the enfeoffment.

Sixth, the name "Sa Fali" appears not to be a Han surname and name; this library does not decide her descent nor rewrite the name.

Seventh, this entry rests on a single notice in the Chaste and Staunch biography of juan 7, and this library has nothing further to add; the volume used here is an online transcription, not checked against a punctuated edition or the printed text, so citations must be marked as from the transcription.
