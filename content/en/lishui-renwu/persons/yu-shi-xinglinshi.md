Madam Yu is one of the figures in the Song section of the "Chaste and Staunch" biography in juan 7 of the Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*. Her notice has only three sentences: her place, her death in refusing dishonour, and the later renaming of her market together with a note on to whom the place belonged. This entry keeps those sentences apart and sets out the gazetteer's own remark on the place'sbelonging.

## The gazetteer text

> 余氏，杏林市人。少寡，里中惡少慕其色，脅以刃，余不從而死。事聞於朝，改市曰節婦里，今屬高淳。
> — Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, juan 7, Chaste and Staunch biography

## Her place and station

"Madam Yu" (Yu shi) is the designation the gazetteer gives; only a surname is recorded, no personal name or courtesy name, so this entry keeps the gazetteer's "Madam Yu" as its title. "Of Xinglin Market" is the residence the notice supplies, and it is the only geographical information here. "Widowed young" is the gazetteer's statement of her early widowhood; who her husband was and in what year he died are nowhere recorded.

## Her death in refusing dishonour

"A ruffian of the neighbourhood coveted her looks and threatened her with a blade" is the gazetteer's account of how the affair began: a ruffian of the same neighbourhood, drawn by her beauty, came at her with a knife. "She refused and died" is the gazetteer's account of her death: she would not submit and was killed for it. How she died and what became of the attacker are not recorded, and nothing is supplied here. The words are the gazetteer's own and are reproduced as its account.

## The later renaming, and the "now belongs to Gaochun" remark

"The matter reached the court, and the market was renamed 'Virtuous Widow Village'" is the gazetteer's account of what followed: her deed was reported to the court, and the place was renamed from a "market" to "Virtuous Widow Village" in her honour. In what year this was done, and on whose memorial, is not recorded. "Now belongs to Gaochun" is the gazetteer's own note on the place'sbelonging at the time of compilation. Two points follow: this is the gazetteer's own annotation and not this library's judgement of jurisdiction; and this library neither infers the place's present administrative position from it nor uses it to reconstruct the details of the place's history in Lishui.

## Relation to Lishui

The gazetteer records her as of Xinglin Market, and she enters this site with the relation "active in Lishui". The reasoning must be stated: her township is the place of residence the gazetteer gives, not a birth record, so "born in Lishui" does not apply; the gazetteer records no office she held in Lishui, no writings of hers, and no recommendation of her out of Lishui, so "official service", "writings" and "recommended from Lishui" do not apply; what is recorded is her keeping of her chastity and her death in refusing dishonour in Lishui, and her neighbourhood's renaming for her, which is her activity in Lishui, so the relation taken is "active in Lishui". The gazetteer's remark that the place "now belongs to Gaochun" is a point the reader must keep in mind: at the time of compilation the place was no longer part of Lishui, so what this entry records is the Lishui of an earlier day.

## Points of doubt

First, her birth and death years are not recorded; "widowed young" states only her early widowhood, and her husband's name and the year she was widowed are nowhere given.

Second, neither her personal name nor her courtesy name is recorded, and the entry stands under the gazetteer's own form, Madam Yu.

Third, her birth place and native place are not explicitly recorded: the gazetteer gives only that she was "of Xinglin Market", her place of residence; to what township Xinglin Market belonged and where her house stood this library has no material.

Fourth, in what year Xinglin Market was renamed "Virtuous Widow Village", and on whose memorial, is not recorded, and nothing is inferred from other juan.

Fifth, "now belongs to Gaochun" is the gazetteer's own note on the place'sbelonging; this library does not infer the place's present administrative position from it, nor compute the year of the division.

Sixth, her family beyond her husband, and any offspring, are not recorded, and nothing is supplied.

Seventh, this entry rests on a single notice in the Chaste and Staunch biography of juan 7, and this library has nothing further to add; the volume used here is an online transcription, not checked against a punctuated edition or the printed text, so citations must be marked as from the transcription.
