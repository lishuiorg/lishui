Liu Wenju is a representative bearer this library found in both district-level documents that call themselves the "third batch". The two rosters are not the same, so this entry reports what each document says, without merging them and without deciding which roster is the official one.

## List status

**The 2017 document.** The notice of the Lishui District People's Government publishing the third batch of district-level protected sites, intangible cultural heritage projects and representative transmitters (Li Zheng Fa [2017] No. 197) has an annex 3 headed "list of representative transmitters (19 in total)". This library read 14 names in it; Liu Wenju is one of them, written as "Liu Wenju (Mingjue pickled greens / wind-dried fish)". That annex gives only the name and the item name, not a birth month or an address.

**The 2021 document.** The notice of the General Office of the Lishui District People's Government publishing the third batch of district-level representative intangible cultural heritage items and bearers (Li Zheng Ban Fa [2021] No. 3) states in its text that six representative items and nine representative bearers were assessed, naming Rui Yongmei, Liu Wenju and Yang Baihai among the bearers. Its annex 2, headed "third batch of district-level intangible cultural heritage representative bearer roster (9 in total)", gives her row as:

> 刘文菊｜1961年8月｜明觉香菜/风鱼｜传统技艺｜石湫街道明觉社区

## The item she is listed under

The two documents write her item name differently: the 2017 annex 3 has one form, the 2021 annex 2 another, with the item category "traditional craft". Both are quoted as written; this entry does not split the combined name and does not rewrite it.

Annex 2 of the 2017 document, the district intangible cultural heritage list of 22 items, carries two separate item names, rendered in this library's glossary as Mingjue Pickled Greens and Mingjue Wind-dried Fish Making. Annex 1 of the 2021 document lists six representative items — Baishiguan horse lantern, Dacun horse lantern, Qingxu horse lantern, maltose making, bamboo weaving and the New-Country shrimp lantern — and does not include the wind-dried fish item. This library holds separate entries for the two items.

## Relation to Lishui

The 2021 annex 2 gives her "unit or address" as Mingjue Community in Shiqiu Subdistrict, quoted as written, with the community name unchanged. A bearer roster evidences transmitting activity in Lishui, not place of birth, so this entry's `relation` is "activity".

## Points of doubt

First, the roster gives her item as one line, "Mingjue pickled greens / wind-dried fish". This library cannot conclude from it that she is a representative bearer of both the pickled-greens item and the wind-dried-fish item; neither document divides her transmitting scope item by item.

Second, both documents call themselves the third batch while their rosters differ. This library does not decide which roster is official and does not add their counts together. Annex 3 of the 2017 document claims 19 bearers, but this library read only 14; the names it did not read are neither written nor supplied.

Third, the birth month "August 1961" appears only in the 2021 document; the 2017 annex 3 gives no birth month and no address. The value is transcribed as written and is not converted into an age.

Fourth, the two item names in annex 2 of the 2017 document are the short forms as written there; their full official names and numbers in the municipal or provincial lists fall outside the scope of these two documents.

Fifth, her sex, native place, place of birth, teachers and lineage, life story and present situation are recorded in none of the sources this library holds.
