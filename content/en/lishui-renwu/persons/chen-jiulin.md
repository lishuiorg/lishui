Chen Jiulin has one record in this library, in Annex 3 of the 2017 notice of the People's Government of Lishui District. This entry states what that record says and why the other document does not contain him.

## Registry status

**Li Zheng Fa [2017] No. 197 (2017).** The notice of the People's Government of Lishui District on publishing the third batch of district-level protected sites, district-level intangible cultural heritage projects and district-level transmitters is dated 22 November 2017 and was posted on the district government website in October 2018; the batch year follows the year carried by the document number. Its Annex 3 is headed "List of representative transmitters (19 persons in total)". This library verified 14 name entries in it, among them 陈九林（面塑）, the name followed by the project name in parentheses. The annex gives name and project only, with no birth date, no project category and no address.

**Li Zheng Ban Fa [2021] No. 3 (2021).** The notice of the General Office of the People's Government of Lishui District on publishing the third batch of district-level representative projects and representative transmitters is dated 11 January 2021. Its Annex 2 is headed "Third batch of district-level intangible cultural heritage representative transmitters (9 persons in total)", and this library transcribed all nine rows of that table; none of them carries the name Chen Jiulin. This entry does not build a second record from that document, and it does not treat the absence as a denial of the record in Annex 3.

Both documents label themselves the third batch while attaching lists of different scope. This entry reports separately what each one records, does not merge them, does not add their counts, and does not decide which list is the formal one.

## The transmitted project

The project name in the 2017 document is written 面塑 (dough figurine making). Neither document gives a project category for it. The source card states that names of this kind — 面塑, 泥塑, 藤匠, 中医 — are the annex's own wording and that their full formal project names still have to be checked against the annex text, so this entry does not infer a formal name from the short one. This library has no entry for the dough figurine project, so this entry points to no project entry and creates none. The materials, technique and subject matter of the craft, and what this person does within it, are recorded in neither document.

## Relation to Lishui

Annex 3 of the 2017 document has no "unit or address" column, and Annex 2 of the 2021 document has one but does not reach this person, so this library holds no address for him in Lishui. The one thing the records do locate is that a Lishui District register of district-level intangible cultural heritage transmitters lists him, which evidences transmitting activity in Lishui and not place of birth. The `relation` value is therefore 活动 (active in Lishui) rather than 本地出生.

## Points of doubt

First, Annex 3 states 19 persons in total, while this library verified only 14 name entries. The names it did not verify are neither written nor supplied here, and the stated total is not used to reconstruct the list.

Second, the two documents claim the same batch while attaching different lists — a 19-person transmitter list against a 9-person transmitter register, issued by different bodies. Which one is the formal register is not recorded in the sources; this entry does not decide, and does not merge or add up the counts.

Third, neither document gives a birth month or an address for this person. This entry does not convert anything into an age and does not supply an address.

Fourth, sex, native place, place of birth, teaching lineage, life events, honours and the present state of transmitting are recorded in neither document. This entry does not guess and does not fill the gaps with common sense.

Fifth, 面塑 is the annex's own wording, and its full formal project name still has to be checked against the annex text. This library has no entry for that project and this entry does not create one. The annex separately records 杨定兴（泥塑）, a different name and a different project name, and the two must not be substituted for each other.
