Rui Yongmei is a representative bearer this library found in both district-level documents that call themselves the "third batch". The two rosters are not the same, so this entry reports what each document says, without merging them and without deciding which roster is the official one.

## List status

**The 2017 document.** The notice of the Lishui District People's Government publishing the third batch of district-level protected sites, intangible cultural heritage projects and representative transmitters (Li Zheng Fa [2017] No. 197) has an annex 3 headed "list of representative transmitters (19 in total)". This library read 14 names in it; Rui Yongmei is one of them, written as "Rui Yongmei (Rui family hand-torn chicken)". That annex gives only the name and the item name, not a birth month or an address.

**The 2021 document.** The notice of the General Office of the Lishui District People's Government publishing the third batch of district-level representative intangible cultural heritage items and bearers (Li Zheng Ban Fa [2021] No. 3) states in its text that six representative items and nine representative bearers were assessed — naming Rui Yongmei, Liu Wenju and Yang Baihai among the bearers. Its annex 2, headed "third batch of district-level intangible cultural heritage representative bearer roster (9 in total)", gives her row as:

> 芮咏梅｜1966年4月｜芮氏手抓鸡制作技艺｜传统技艺｜洪蓝街道西旺社区

## The item she transmits

The 2017 annex 3 gives the item name in a short form, while annex 2 of the same document — the district intangible cultural heritage list of 22 items — carries the longer form, rendered in this library's glossary as Rui Family Hand-torn Chicken. The 2021 annex 2 gives the item name in that longer form as well, with the item category "traditional craft". All three are transcribed as written; this entry neither expands them nor rewrites them. This library holds a separate entry for the item itself.

## Relation to Lishui

The 2021 annex 2 gives her "unit or address" as Xiwang Community in Honglan Subdistrict, quoted as written, with the community name unchanged. A bearer roster evidences transmitting activity in Lishui, not place of birth, so this entry's `relation` is "activity".

## Points of doubt

First, both documents call themselves the third batch while their rosters differ. This library does not decide which roster is official and does not add their counts together. Annex 3 of the 2017 document claims 19 bearers, but this library read only 14; the names it did not read are neither written nor supplied.

Second, within the same 2017 document annex 3 uses the short item name and annex 2 the longer one. This entry records both as written and does not decide which is the formal name; the formal full name of the item has to be checked against the annex itself and was not reached by this library.

Third, the birth month "April 1966" appears only in the 2021 document; the 2017 annex 3 gives no birth month and no address. The value is transcribed as written and is not converted into an age.

Fourth, her sex, native place, place of birth, teachers and lineage, life story and present situation are recorded in none of the sources this library holds.
