Madam Xie is one of the chaste widows of Lishui recorded in juan 7, the "Chaste and Martyred Women" biography, of the Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*. The gazetteer gives only her natal surname Xie, her husband Xiao Bishou, and her widowhood, service to her parents-in-law, raising of her orphan, and two honours. The passage sits under a heading "Sojourners"; this library judges that word a suspect heading, as explained in its own section below. This entry rests on a single item in this gazetteer and adds nothing the text does not record.

## The gazetteer text

> 謝氏，蕭必壽之妻。年二十，子生甫八月即喪夫。善事舅姑，矢志撫孤。知縣閔派魯、縣令李作楫俱扁額旌表之。
> —— 康熙《溧水县志》卷之七·贞烈传

## Reading

The item is layered.

"Madam Xie, wife of Xiao Bishou" gives her natal surname and her husband's name. Her given name, courtesy name, residence, and paternal family are all unrecorded, as are her husband's courtesy name and status.

"At twenty, eight months after her son's birth, she lost her husband" gives her age and her child's infancy: widowed at twenty, when her son was barely eight months old. The "just eight months" shows how young the child was.

"She served her parents-in-law well and vowed to raise her orphan" gives two things: serving her parents-in-law well, and vowing to raise the orphan. The vow and the raising are recorded; the number of years of her chastity and her son's name are unrecorded.

"The magistrates Min Pailu and Li Zuoyi both honoured her with plaques" gives two honours and their form: Min Pailu and Li Zuoyi, both magistrates of this county, honoured her with plaques. The two are named together, showing two successive honours; the word "both" marks the pairing. The plaque texts and the years of the honours are unrecorded.

## The suspect heading "Sojourners"

The passage this item belongs to carries a heading "Sojourners" in the transcription. This library judges the word a suspect heading: what follows (Cheng Tianmin's daughter, the Chen daughter, Wu Ai, Madam Ma, and others) are still chaste widows and martyred women, and does not match the sense of "sojourners". This library keeps the word as recorded and marks it a suspect heading, and does not read this item as belonging to a sojourner biography; whether the passage continues the Chaste and Martyred Women biography or is another biography must be settled against the printed edition.

## Relation to Lishui

This entry enters her as "active in Lishui", and the reason must be stated. What the gazetteer records is her chastity in Lishui: wife of Xiao Bishou, keeping chastity from twenty, serving her parents-in-law, and raising her orphan, honoured with plaques successively by the county magistrates Min Pailu and Li Zuoyi — her community her home and the honouring officials Lishui officers. But the gazetteer records no birthplace or native place for her, naming her only "Madam Xie", so this entry does not record her as "born locally". There is no office, recommendation, or writing to attach to her, so "official service", "recommended from Lishui", and "writings" do not apply; since the record is of keeping her home, not of lodging abroad, "sojourn" does not apply either. Of the five categories only "active in Lishui" holds, and this entry uses it.

## Points of doubt

First, her birth and death years are unrecorded. The gazetteer gives only that she was widowed at twenty; it gives no year of birth, no year for her husband, no number of years of chastity, and no year of death, and this entry converts and infers nothing.

Second, her birthplace and native place are not explicitly recorded. The gazetteer calls her only "Madam Xie"; her paternal family and residence are unrecorded.

Third, beyond her husband's name, her paternal family and offspring are not fully recorded. The gazetteer gives only her husband Xiao Bishou; his courtesy name and status are unrecorded, as are the orphan's name and later career.

Fourth, only the honouring officials and the plaque form are recorded, not the plaque texts or the years of the two honours. The gazetteer names Min Pailu and Li Zuoyi together with the word "both"; this entry keeps that wording without judging their order or whether they refer to a single affair.

Fifth, the item is brief, with no detail beyond her widowhood, her service to her parents-in-law, her raising of the orphan, and the honours, and this entry does not draw on other items to fill it out.

Sixth, this entry rests on a single item in this gazetteer, in the "Sojourners" (suspect heading) block of the Chaste and Martyred Women biography; the volume is an online transcription, not checked against a punctuated edition or the printed text, so citations must be marked as from the transcription.

Seventh, searches across the other source layers of this library produced nothing that could add to her.
