Tang Shen is one of the Song officials of Lishui listed in juan 6 of the Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*. His biography is among the shortest of the Song entries in the volume: one line of origin, one year of appointment, one sentence of praise. This entry records only what that line records, and supplies no life for him.

## Name and origin

> 汤诜，字伯行，丞相思退之孙。嘉定二年任，存心仁恕，民共戴之，见于涂歌。
> — Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, juan 6

He was courtesy-named Boxing, and the gazetteer calls him "grandson of the chancellor Situi" — here the gazetteer drops the surname and refers to him only as "the chancellor". This entry transcribes the wording as it stands and does not restore the name. He was appointed to Lishui in the second year of the Jiading era.

## What the record says about his Lishui term

Everything the biography says about his term is the single sentence "benevolent and forgiving in heart, the people all honoured him, and this was heard in the songs on the roads". "Benevolent and forgiving in heart" is the gazetteer's judgement; "the people all honoured him, heard in the songs on the roads" is its account of popular reputation — road songs are talk of popular reputation, not a verifiable record. Both are quoted and marked as such, and no act, building or fiscal measure is supplied from them.

## Relation to Lishui

He was not a Lishui native and enters this library through official service. What ties him to the county is the statement of his appointment and the reputation attributed to him during it.

## Points of doubt

First, his birth and death years and his native place are not recorded.

Second, the gazetteer drops the surname of the chancellor it names; this entry neither restores the name nor describes that man's career.

Third, only the year of arrival is given; the year he left Lishui is not recorded.

Fourth, the praise and the popular reputation are the gazetteer's judgement and narration, and what they rested on is not recorded.

Fifth, any concrete act in Lishui, his later career, his final rank and his place of death are all unrecorded.

Sixth, this entry rests on one biography in this volume; searches across the other source layers of this library produced nothing that could add to him.
