Yang Guozhen is one of the Qing officials of Lishui listed in juan 6 of the Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*. His biography is short and records a single matter — his presence at the granary during the transfer of tribute grain — the rest being judgement.

## Origin and term

> 杨国桢，字□□，辽东景州人。由旗下贡，顺治三年任。
> — Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, juan 6

He was a native of Jingzhou in Liaodong, entered as a tribute student of the banners, and was appointed to Lishui in the third year of the Shunzhi era. His courtesy name appears in the transcription as blank squares; this entry keeps them exactly as they stand and supplies no character. The gazetteer does not explain the administrative relation between "Liaodong" and "Jingzhou", and this entry transcribes both as they stand; what "tribute student of the banners" precisely means is unrecorded. The gazetteer writes only "appointed" and gives no office title.

**"Our dynasty" means the Qing.** This volume lists Yang Guozhen together with Luo Jiashi, Wang Dingyin and An Yingsui under the heading "our dynasty". The gazetteer was written by men of the Kangxi period, who called the Qing "our dynasty"; this entry treats him as a Qing official and notes the original wording.

## What the record says about his acts

**Judgements.**

> 为人廉明爽直，兼长武略，视事快便，据案批驳，无弗中节。
> — Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, juan 6

"Incorrupt, clear, frank and direct", "gifted also in military matters", "quick and efficient in handling business", "never off the mark when he ruled on papers at his desk" are all the gazetteer's judgements, naming no act.

**Transfer of tribute grain.**

> 兑运漕粮亲临仓所，与运官运役争执，升合不得亏损粮长，十二乡莫不感戴。
> — Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, juan 6

When grain was being transferred for the tribute-fleet transport he went in person to the granary station and disputed with the transport officers and the transport labourers, letting not a *sheng* or a *ge* be lost to the grain-heads. "None of the twelve townships failed to be grateful" is the gazetteer's reported praise; "twelve townships" is the number of townships the gazetteer gives, and their names and the basis of the count are unrecorded, as are the location of the granary station, the quantity of grain and the year of the transfer.

**Promotion away and final rank.**

> 无何擢去，官至监司。
> — Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, juan 6

Before long he was promoted away and rose to a supervisory office. The year of his departure and the specific office meant by "supervisory office" are both unrecorded.

## Relation to Lishui

He was not a Lishui native and enters this library through official service. What ties him to the county is his appointment in the third year of the Shunzhi era, his personal presence at the granary during the transfer of tribute grain, and the reported gratitude of the twelve townships. This entry is, within what this library knows, the most direct account of tribute-grain transfer in Lishui among the biographies of this volume.

## Points of doubt

First, his birth and death years are unrecorded.

Second, his courtesy name appears as blank squares in the transcription and is kept as it stands.

Third, the administrative relation between "Liaodong" and "Jingzhou", and the meaning of "tribute student of the banners", are unrecorded.

Fourth, his departure is given only as "before long he was promoted away"; the era year gives only his arrival, and nothing is converted.

Fifth, his Lishui office is given only as "appointed", with no precise title.

Sixth, the specific office and the date of his final supervisory rank are unrecorded.

Seventh, the location of the granary station, the quantity of grain and the year of the transfer are unrecorded, as are the names of the twelve townships and the basis of that count.

Eighth, "incorrupt, clear, frank and direct", "gifted in military matters", "never off the mark" and "none of the twelve townships failed to be grateful" are the gazetteer's judgements and reported praise.

Ninth, this volume lists him under "our dynasty", meaning the Qing; the original wording is noted above.

Tenth, this entry rests on one biography in this volume, which is an online transcription not checked against the punctuated edition or the paper book; searches across the other source layers of this library produced nothing that could add to him.
