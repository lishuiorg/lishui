Zhu Runzu is one of the men presented from Lishui listed in the "Ming, Hongwu period" block of the Recommendations examination in juan 2 of the Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*. The section gives him two items — the route of presentation and the office held — and nothing else. This entry writes only those two items and supplies no life for him.

## Route into office and office held

> 朱潤祖由明經，任本縣訓導。
> — Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, juan 2

"By way of knowledge of the classics" is the category of presentation as the gazetteer words it; this entry reproduces it and does not convert it into "jinshi" or any other degree, and offers no account of how this category was selected or administered — the section says nothing of it. "Served as an assistant instructor of the county" is the only line in this block whose office carries the words "of the county", and it is the only statement known to this library that could place one of his posts in Lishui. Yet the line never states which county it means, and that is all the section says: since this is a Lishui gazetteer's roster, "the county" is most likely Lishui, but this entry reproduces the two words and marks the uncertainty rather than deciding the question. What an assistant instructor did, and when he took up and left the post, is nowhere recorded. This block gives no years at all, so neither the year of presentation nor the year of appointment can be known.

## Relation to Lishui

The gazetteer gives no native place and records no act of his in Lishui. He enters the persons site with the relation "Recommended from Lishui", and the reasoning must be stated: the only tie the section makes between him and Lishui is that he was presented from Lishui — being named in the Recommendations examination of a Lishui gazetteer is itself the gazetteer's statement that he was recommended out of Lishui. This value is used only for this section, and differs from the others: the section records no birthplace, so "born in Lishui" is not available; although the words "of the county" could point to a Lishui post, the line does not say which county, so this entry does not decide that he held a Lishui post and does not use "official service"; the section records no activity of his in Lishui, so "active in Lishui" is not available. The tag "Education and culture" is given here only because the office he held was a school post, and implies no further educational activity of his in Lishui.

## Points of doubt

First, his birth and death years, his native place and his courtesy name are nowhere recorded.

Second, the era value "Ming" here only records that the section places him in its "Ming, Hongwu period" block. That grouping dates his presentation and service, not his birth: it proves neither that he was born in the Hongwu era nor that he was active in any particular Hongwu year, and this block gives no years at all.

Third, his route into office is given only as "by way of knowledge of the classics", which must not be rewritten as "jinshi" or any other degree; "assistant instructor of the county" is an office title whose duties and term are unrecorded.

Fourth, which county "the county" means is this entry's largest single uncertainty. The section gives no county name and this library holds no material outside this section to check it against, so whether his post lay in Lishui is not decided, and the relation stays "Recommended from Lishui" rather than "official service".

Fifth, whether he had a house, descendants or remains in Lishui is nowhere recorded, and this library has no material to decide it.

Sixth, this entry rests on one roster line in the Hongwu block; searches across the other source layers of this library produced nothing that could add to him.
