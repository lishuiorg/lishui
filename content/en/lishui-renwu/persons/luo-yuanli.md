Luo Yuanli is one of the men presented from Lishui and served elsewhere listed in the "Ming, Yongle period" block of the Recommendations examination in juan 2 of the Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*. That section is a roster rather than a biography: each line gives a name, sometimes a courtesy name or one clause of kinship, the route into office, and the office held, and gives no account of the man. This entry holds only "presented as a talented man" and "served as county vice-magistrate", and is among the shortest in the section; everything the gazetteer does not record is placed under Points of doubt rather than supplied.

## Route of presentation and office held

> 駱元禮由人材，任縣丞。
> — Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, juan 2

"By way of talented men" is the category of presentation as the gazetteer words it; this entry reproduces the two characters and does not convert them into "jinshi" or any other degree. "Served as county vice-magistrate" is the office as the gazetteer words it, written in two characters with no county attached. Other lines of the same section do name a county — the vice-magistrate of Qishan County in Shaanxi — but this entry borrows no county from them and supplies no duties or term. This block gives no years, so neither the year of presentation nor the year of taking up office can be known.

## What the gazetteer records, and the limit of this entry

The section gives no life: it records no native place, no courtesy name, who presented him or by what procedure, what he did in whatever county he served, how he was promoted, what his final office was, or anything after his death. This entry reasons from the words "talented men" and "county vice-magistrate" to no institutional detail, since the gazetteer records none.

## Relation to Lishui

The gazetteer gives no native place and records no act of his in Lishui. He enters the persons site with the relation "Recommended from Lishui", and the reasoning must be stated: the only tie the section makes between him and Lishui is that he was presented from Lishui and served elsewhere — being named in the Recommendations examination of a Lishui gazetteer is itself the gazetteer's statement that he was recommended out of Lishui and held office away from it. This value is used only for this section, and differs from the others: the section records no birthplace, so "born in Lishui" is not available; his post is not placed in Lishui and the gazetteer records no Lishui post for him, so "official service" is not available; the section records no activity of his in Lishui, so "active in Lishui" is not available.

## Points of doubt

First, his birth and death years, his native place and his courtesy name are nowhere recorded.

Second, the era value "Ming" here only records that the section places him in its "Ming, Yongle period" block. That grouping dates his presentation and service, not his birth: it proves neither that he was born in the Yongle era nor that he was active in any particular Yongle year, and this block gives no years at all.

Third, his route into office is given only as "by way of talented men", which must not be rewritten as "jinshi" or any other degree; the office is a bare title, the county is not named, and its duties and term are unrecorded.

Fourth, whether he had a house, descendants or remains in Lishui is nowhere recorded, and this library has no material to decide it.

Fifth, the same Yongle block carries another line ending as a county vice-magistrate, for a man presented through a different category and serving in a named county; sharing an office title proves nothing about a link between them, and this entry infers none.

Sixth, this entry rests on one roster line; searches across the other source layers of this library produced nothing that could add to him.
