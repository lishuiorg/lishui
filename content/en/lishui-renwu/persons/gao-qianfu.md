Gao Qianfu is one of the Ming officials of Lishui listed in juan 6 of the Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*. His biography is very short: one clause of native place, one of appointment, three of praise. This entry records only that, and supplies no life for him.

## Origin and term of appointment

> 高谦甫，温州人。洪武五年任
> — Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, juan 6

He was a native of Wenzhou and was appointed in the fifth year of the Hongwu era. This biography never uses the word "Lishui"; his service there follows from the volume itself, which lists the officials of Lishui. The gazetteer also never gives his title in Lishui, and this entry does not assume that of county magistrate.

## What the gazetteer records

> 征收不扰，差役均平，以廉能称。
> — Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, juan 6

These three clauses are the whole of what the biography says about his term. "Levying without disturbing" and "corvée evenly balanced" are the gazetteer's account of the character of his collection of taxes and labour service; it records no method, no register, no quota, and how the balance was achieved. "He was praised for integrity and ability" is praise attributed to him by the gazetteer. All three are quoted and marked as such, and no fiscal measure, register or quota is supplied from them.

## Relation to Lishui

He was not a Lishui native — the gazetteer says "a native of Wenzhou" — and enters this library through official service. What ties him to the county is the statement of his appointment and the reputation attributed to him during it. The tag "Taxation and corvée" answers only to the two clauses on collection and labour service, not to any recorded fiscal installation, register or quota.

## Points of doubt

First, his birth and death years are not recorded; his native place is given only as Wenzhou.

Second, precision of the term: "the fifth year of the Hongwu era" is a definite year in this volume and covers only his arrival; the year he left Lishui is not recorded, and nothing is converted into a Common Era year.

Third, his title in Lishui is not given, and this entry does not write it as county magistrate.

Fourth, "levying without disturbing, corvée evenly balanced" is the gazetteer's narration and judgement; the method, registers and quotas behind it are nowhere recorded.

Fifth, "he was praised for integrity and ability" is praise reported by the gazetteer, and what it rested on is not recorded.

Sixth, any concrete act in Lishui, his later career, his final rank and his place of death are all unrecorded.

Seventh, this entry rests on one biography in this volume, an online transcription not checked against a punctuated or printed edition; searches across the other source layers of this library produced nothing that could add to him.
