Luan Shangyue is one of the Ming officials of Lishui listed in juan 6 of the Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*. His biography is short: one line of origin and appointment, one line of judgement and popular memory, one line listing a text and two steles. This entry records only what that line records and supplies no list of his policies.

## Origin and term

> 栾尚约，胶州人。由进士，嘉靖三十年任。
> — Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, juan 6

He was a native of Jiaozhou, entered as a *jinshi*, and was appointed to Lishui in the thirtieth year of the Jiajing era. The gazetteer gives no office title here, only "appointed"; the era year gives only his arrival, not the year he left, and this entry converts nothing into a common-era year.

## What the record says about his acts

**Administration and posthumous reputation.**

> 在官多惠政，百姓怀之。凡所修葺迄今皆指为栾公遗泽，爱比甘棠。
> — Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, juan 6

"In office he gave many beneficial policies, and the common people kept him in their hearts" is the gazetteer's judgement, naming no act. "Whatever he had built or repaired was, down to the present, all pointed to as the leftover benefit of Lord Luan" is the gazetteer's account of posthumous reputation: the "down to the present" here means the time of the Kangxi compilation, more than a century after the thirtieth year of the Jiajing era, and is not a statement about today. "Loved in the manner of the sweet pear tree" alludes to the legend of the earl who heard cases beneath a pear tree and whose tree the people loved too much to cut; it is the gazetteer's figure of speech, not a record. What he built or repaired, where, and in which year — all unrecorded.

**His text and two steles.**

> 有《县宰题名记》暨正显、东岳庙碑，见《艺文志》。
> — Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, juan 6

The gazetteer lists titles and a location only: one record of the county magistrates' names, and two temple steles, with the note that they are to be found in this gazetteer's arts and literature section. This library has not checked that section, so this entry supplies no text of the record or the steles, does not judge whether either survives, and does not infer from the four characters naming the two temples which temples they belong to or when the steles were set up.

## Relation to Lishui

He was not a Lishui native and enters this library through official service. What ties him to the county is his appointment in the thirtieth year of the Jiajing era, the building work later remembered as his leftover benefit, and the text and two steles listed under his name in this gazetteer's arts and literature section — the last being, within what this library knows, the only layer in which his name can be attached to Lishui documents and construction.

## Points of doubt

First, his birth and death years are not recorded.

Second, the year he left office is not recorded; the era year gives only his arrival, and this entry neither converts it nor infers the length of his term.

Third, the gazetteer gives no office title for his Lishui post, only "appointed"; this entry does not write him as a county magistrate.

Fourth, "many beneficial policies" and "whatever he had built or repaired" are the gazetteer's judgement and summary; the content, place and date of each item are unrecorded.

Fifth, "down to the present" means the Kangxi compilation, and this entry marks it as such rather than turning it into a claim that anything still stands; whether the two steles and the record survived to the Kangxi period or survive today is unrecorded.

Sixth, the full text, the juan placement, the dates and the present state of the record of magistrates' names and of the two temple steles are not supplied, because this library has not verified this gazetteer's arts and literature section.

Seventh, the sweet-pear-tree comparison is the gazetteer's allusion, not a record.

Eighth, this entry rests on one biography in this volume, which is an online transcription not checked against the punctuated edition or the paper book; searches across the other source layers of this library produced nothing that could add to him.
