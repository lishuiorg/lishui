Zheng Yunlong is one of the seven men in the "Song" block of the Filial Sons biography in juan 7 of the Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*. His line is a single clause: a name and an act of filiality. This is the shortest entry of the block, and this entry writes only what that clause records, supplying no life for him.

## What the gazetteer records him doing

> 鄭雲龍，割股療母疾。
> — Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, juan 7

Cutting flesh from the thigh is an act of filiality as the gazetteer records it; this entry reproduces the gazetteer's wording and marks its nature: it is the gazetteer's account, and this entry judges neither its medical plausibility nor offers a modern explanation, and presents it as no verifiable fact. The gazetteer does not say what illness his mother had, whether the cure succeeded, or in what year it happened.

## No residence and no commendation are recorded

This is one of the lines of the Song block that gives no place: the gazetteer records no township, no village, no house, and no birthplace for him. It is also one of the two lines that record no commendation, like the line on Xie Qianjiu: no year, no word on any report to the court, no commendatory name. This entry therefore writes no residence section and no commendation section, and infers from the pattern of neighbouring lines — their place-names of the town, Qiqiao, Shanyang and Sihé townships, or their Qiandao, Chunxi and Jiading dates — nothing about this man's residence or any commendation of him.

## Relation to Lishui

The gazetteer records no residence for him. He enters the persons site with the relation "Active in Lishui", and the reasoning must be stated: he stands in a Lishui gazetteer's Filial Sons biography, and his tie to Lishui is that placement together with the filial act the biography records, yet the biography records no birthplace and no township for him, so "born in Lishui" is not available; the gazetteer records no Lishui office, no writing, and no recommendation out of Lishui into office elsewhere, so "official service", "writings" and "Recommended from Lishui" are not available; what it records is his filial conduct, which is activity as this library knows it, so this entry uses "Active in Lishui". The limit of that choice must also be stated: since the biography gives him no residence, "activity" here reaches only as far as the biography's placement of him and the act it records, and reaches no particular place in Lishui.

## Points of doubt

First, his birth and death years are nowhere recorded, no residence is recorded, no family is recorded, and neither his mother's illness nor the outcome of the cure is recorded.

Second, this line records no year at all: neither the year of the act nor any commendation year, and this entry infers none from neighbouring lines and converts nothing.

Third, no residence is recorded: township, village and house are all unrecorded, and this library has no material to decide them; the relation "Active in Lishui" reaches only the biography's placement of him and not any named locality.

Fourth, cutting flesh from the thigh is the gazetteer's account, and its nature is marked in the body of this entry; this entry judges no plausibility and presents it as no modern fact.

Fifth, the order of the seven Song lines does not follow the era years the block gives, since this line and the line on Xie Qianjiu record no year at all; the order therefore cannot be used as evidence of sequence, and this entry infers no dates from it.
