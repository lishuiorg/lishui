Xie Qianjiu is one of the seven men in the "Song" block of the Filial Sons biography in juan 7 of the Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*. His line is two clauses: a residence and an act of filiality. The biography records no birth or death years and no family, and this entry writes only what this line records, supplying no life for him.

## Where the gazetteer places him

> 謝千九，山陽鄉人。
> — Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, juan 7

"Shanyang Township" is one of the township names this biography records and the only place-name this line gives: he lived in Shanyang Township, and the gazetteer records no village, no house, and no birthplace. This library has no material to decide where Shanyang Township lay or what it corresponds to, and this entry supplies and infers nothing.

## What the gazetteer records him doing

> 割股療父疾。
> — Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, juan 7

Cutting flesh from the thigh is an act of filiality as the gazetteer records it; this entry reproduces the gazetteer's wording and marks its nature: it is the gazetteer's account, and this entry judges neither its medical plausibility nor offers a modern explanation, and presents it as no verifiable fact. The gazetteer does not say what illness his father had, whether the cure succeeded, or in what year it happened.

## No commendation is recorded

This is one of the lines of the Song block that records no commendation: the gazetteer gives no year here, says nothing about any report to the court, and names no commendation. This entry therefore writes no commendation section and infers from the pattern of neighbouring lines — those dated to the Qiandao, Chunxi and Jiading eras — no commendation for this man.

## Relation to Lishui

The gazetteer places him as "a man of Shanyang Township", which is a residence, not a birth record. He enters the persons site with the relation "Active in Lishui", and the reasoning must be stated: the locality the biography gives is where a man lived, not where he was born — the biography records no birthplace for anyone, only residence and where the act took place, so "born in Lishui" is not available; the gazetteer records no Lishui office, no writing, and no recommendation out of Lishui into office elsewhere, so "official service", "writings" and "Recommended from Lishui" are not available; what it records is that he cured his father's illness in Shanyang Township, which is activity in Lishui, so this entry uses "Active in Lishui".

## Points of doubt

First, his birth and death years are nowhere recorded, his family is nowhere recorded, and neither his father's illness nor the outcome of the cure is recorded.

Second, this line records no year at all: neither the year of the act nor any commendation year, and this entry infers none from neighbouring lines and converts nothing.

Third, "Shanyang Township" is the biography's word for his residence; where it lay, what it corresponds to, and where his house stood are unrecorded, and this library has no material to decide them.

Fourth, cutting flesh from the thigh is the gazetteer's account, and its nature is marked in the body of this entry; this entry judges no plausibility and presents it as no modern fact.

Fifth, the order of the seven Song lines does not follow the commendation years the block gives, and this line and the line on Zheng Yunlong record no year at all; the order therefore cannot be used as evidence of sequence, and this entry infers no dates from it.
