Madam Zhou is one of the Ming-era chaste women of Lishui in the "Biographies of the Chaste" of juan 7 in the Kangxi Lishui County Gazetteer, recorded as the wife of Yan Guofu. This entry rests on that single notice.

## Gazetteer text

> 周氏，嚴國輔妻。年十九夫死，有遺腹甫數月，即矢死不二。及生男，辛勤撫立。事姑以孝聞，貞節永年，見其曾孫。知縣張錫命旌其廬。
> —— 康熙《溧水县志》卷之七·贞烈传

## Reading of the text

First, "wife of Yan Guofu" names husband and wife.

Second, "her husband died when she was nineteen, leaving her a few months pregnant, and she at once swore never to take a second husband."

Third, "when she bore a son she raised him with toil."

Fourth, "she served her mother-in-law with filial piety that was known, and her chastity endured so that she saw her great-grandson." "Chastity enduring long years" is the gazetteer's phrase, not an exact count.

Fifth, "the magistrate Zhang Ximing honoured her gate." The year, number and form of the honour are not given.

## Relation to Lishui

The gazetteer identifies her only through her husband, Yan Guofu, whose household was her place of residence; her chastity, her filial care and the honour to her gate attach to the Lishui countryside. The text neither states her birthplace nor calls her a native of Lishui, so this entry does not mark her as "native of Lishui." She held no office, received no recommendation and left no writings, so the association is recorded as "active in Lishui," based on her conduct recorded there.

## Points of doubt

First, no birth or death years are given; "nineteen" dates her husband's death, not her birth.

Second, her birthplace and native place are not stated; the gazetteer ties her only to her husband's name.

Third, the life, rank and death year of Yan Guofu are unrecorded.

Fourth, the son she bore is unnamed and his success unrecorded, and the great-grandson she saw is not identified.

Fifth, "chastity enduring long years" is the gazetteer's phrase, not a number of years, and this entry draws no figure from it.

Sixth, the magistrate Zhang Ximing's honour gives no year, number or tablet inscription.

Seventh, this entry rests on one notice in juan 7 of the Kangxi gazetteer, a network transcription not checked against a critical or printed edition.
