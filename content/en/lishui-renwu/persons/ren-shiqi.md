Ren Shiqi is one of the Qing filial sons of Lishui listed in juan 7 of the Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, in its "Filial Sons" biography section. The biography records two deeds and one commendation; its commender already has an entry in this library, so this entry makes clear which text the commendation belongs to.

## The gazetteer text

> 任師琦，年方總角，母疾篤，割股和藥以進，母尋愈，後十五年方卒。老年事父，猶若嬰兒。族黨間稱曰孝子，知縣楊國楨獎之。
> —— 康熙《溧水县志》卷之七·孝子传

## What the gazetteer records

"When still at the top-knots age" is the gazetteer's way of stating his age at the deed — a childhood stage named after a hairstyle, kept as the gazetteer has it, converted to no specific age and used for no birth year. His mother fell gravely ill; he cut his thigh, compounded it into medicine and gave it to her; she soon recovered and only died fifteen years later. The subject of "died after fifteen years" carries over from the mother, and this span is the only time the biography gives; the years at which it began and ended are unrecorded, and this entry converts and infers nothing.

The gazetteer also records that in old age he served his father as an infant does, a second item of conduct, undated, with his father's name, birth and death unrecorded. "His clan called him a filial son" is a reputation the gazetteer reports, a matter of local opinion rather than verifiable record; this entry reproduces it and marks its nature.

## The commender Yang Guozhen: this deed comes from the Filial Sons section, not from his own biography

"The district magistrate Yang Guozhen commended him" is the only honour the biography gives. Three points must be made clear against an existing entry of this library.

First, this library already has an entry for Yang Guozhen (`ls:person:yang-guozhen`), resting on the officials' biography in juan 6, and that biography records no commendation of a filial son. The commending of Ren Shiqi therefore reaches this library only through the Filial Sons section, and this entry records it as such rather than treating it as something Yang Guozhen's own biography says.

Second, the year and form of the commendation are nowhere recorded; this entry does not infer the form from other entries in this volume that mention tablets, archways or grants of grain and cloth, and does not date the matter from any year attached to Yang Guozhen's own biography.

Third, the `related` link to the Yang Guozhen entry is a cross-reference for checking only and merges no deeds of the two men.

## Relation to Lishui

The gazetteer gives Ren Shiqi no township and no native place; it ties him to this county through being named in the filial sons section, through the praise of his clan and through the commendation of a magistrate of this county. This entry enters him as "active in Lishui": what the text proves is activity and commendation in this county, not birth here, so it does not record him as "born locally".

## Points of doubt

First, his birth and death years are not recorded; township and native place are unrecorded; the biography carries no dates, and this entry converts and infers nothing.

Second, "at the top-knots age" is a childhood stage as the gazetteer states it and cannot yield a birth year; this entry converts it to no specific age.

Third, what the mother's illness was, how the cutting and treatment were carried out, and the years of her recovery and death are unrecorded; "fifteen years" gives a span, not its endpoints.

Fourth, his father's name, birth and death and the date of his service in old age are unrecorded; the biography names no other relative.

Fifth, "his clan called him a filial son" is reported local opinion, not fact.

Sixth, the year and form of the commendation are unrecorded; Yang Guozhen's own biography records no commendation of a filial son, and this entry does not merge the two texts.

Seventh, his origin is not recorded, and this entry supplies no examination title.

Eighth, the era field "Qing" here follows the dynasty grouping of the "present dynasty" block of this biography section, not a birth record.

Ninth, this entry rests on a single biography in juan 7; the volume used here is an online transcription, not checked against a punctuated edition or the printed text, so citations must be marked as from the transcription. Searches across the other source layers of this library produced nothing that could add to him.
