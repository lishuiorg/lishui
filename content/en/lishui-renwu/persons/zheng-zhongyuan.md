Zheng Zhongyuan is one of the Ming officials of Lishui listed in juan 6 of the Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*. His biography is very short: one clause of native place, one of appointment, three of judgement. This entry records only that, and supplies no life for him.

## Origin and term of appointment

> 郑仲源，临海人。永乐十年任
> — Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, juan 6

He was a native of Linhai and was appointed in the tenth year of the Yongle era, a definite year in this volume covering only his arrival; nothing is converted into a Common Era year. The biography never uses the word "Lishui"; his service there follows from the volume itself, which lists the officials of Lishui. The gazetteer also never gives his title in Lishui, and this entry does not assume that of county magistrate.

## What the gazetteer records

> 公勤处事，谦让自持，有明敏才而退然不居。
> — Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*, juan 6

These three clauses are the whole of what the biography says about his term, and all three are judgement: he dealt with affairs out of public duty and with diligence, kept himself by deference, and had a quick and clear talent yet withdrew from claiming it. The biography records no building, no case, no fiscal measure and no educational measure in Lishui, and none is inferred from the judgement.

## Relation to Lishui

He was not a Lishui native — the gazetteer says "a native of Linhai" — and enters this library through official service. What ties him to the county is the statement of his appointment and the three clauses of judgement. This entry is all this library has on him.

## Points of doubt

First, his birth and death years are not recorded; his native place is given only as Linhai.

Second, precision of the term: "the tenth year of the Yongle era" is a definite year in this volume and covers only his arrival; the year he left Lishui is not recorded, and nothing is converted into a Common Era year.

Third, his title in Lishui is not given, and this entry does not write it as county magistrate.

Fourth, the three clauses are the gazetteer's judgement, and the acts behind them are nowhere recorded.

Fifth, any concrete act in Lishui, his later career, his final rank and his place of death are all unrecorded.

Sixth, this entry rests on one biography in this volume, an online transcription not checked against a punctuated or printed edition; searches across the other source layers of this library produced nothing that could add to him.
