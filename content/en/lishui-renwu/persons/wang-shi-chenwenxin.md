Madam Wang is one of the Ming-era chaste women of Lishui in the "Biographies of the Chaste" of juan 7 in the Kangxi Lishui County Gazetteer, recorded as the daughter of Wang Han and the second wife of Chen Wenxin. Hers is one of the few notices in the Ming section that names her father. This entry rests on that single record.

## Gazetteer text

> 王氏，王涵女。年十八歲適陳文心為繼室，二十一歲守節，撫孤四十七年。知縣李可埴給扁旌獎「貞操裕後」。順治五年貢生陳雲犀，其子也。
> —— 康熙《溧水县志》卷之七·贞烈传

## Reading of the text

First, "Madam Wang, daughter of Wang Han" gives her paternal family; hers is one of the few notices in the Ming section to preserve a father's name, reproduced here without further reading.

Second, "at eighteen she married Chen Wenxin as his second wife, was widowed at twenty-one, and raised an orphan for forty-seven years" gives her marriage, widowhood and long service as a mother.

Third, "the magistrate Li Kezhi granted her a tablet inscribed 'Chaste Conduct, Blessing Posterity'" records the honour. The office, name and tablet text are reproduced as given.

Fourth, "the tribute student Chen Yunxi of the fifth year of Shunzhi was her son." The reign year is kept as given and not converted.

## Relation to Lishui

The gazetteer identifies her only through her husband, Chen Wenxin, whose household was her place of residence; her chastity, her raising of the orphan and the honour she received attach to the Chen family in Lishui. The text neither states her birthplace nor calls her a native of Lishui, recording only her father Wang Han without his native place, so this entry does not mark her as "native of Lishui." She held no office, received no recommendation and left no writings, so the association is recorded as "active in Lishui," based on her conduct recorded there.

## Points of doubt

First, no birth or death years are given; "eighteen," "twenty-one" and "forty-seven years" are the gazetteer's figures.

Second, her birthplace and native place are not stated; the gazetteer gives only her father's name, and this entry draws no conclusion.

Third, the life, rank and death year of Chen Wenxin are unrecorded, and whether her son Chen Yunxi was her own child or the first wife's is not made clear; the text says only "her son."

Fourth, the fifth year of Shunzhi dates only her son's tribute status; his career beyond that is unrecorded.

Fifth, the only honour recorded is the tablet from the magistrate Li Kezhi; whether there were others is unstated, and "Chaste Conduct, Blessing Posterity" is the tablet text the gazetteer gives.

Sixth, this entry rests on one notice in juan 7 of the Kangxi gazetteer, a network transcription not checked against a critical or printed edition.
