Madam Li is one of the chaste widows of Lishui recorded in juan 7, the "Chaste and Martyred Women" biography, of the Kangxi *Lishui County Gazetteer*. The gazetteer gives only her natal surname Li, her husband Xiao Shilun, and three things: her widowhood, her chastity, and an honour. The passage sits under a heading "Sojourners"; this library judges that word a suspect heading, as explained in its own section below. This entry rests on a single item in this gazetteer and adds nothing the text does not record.

## The gazetteer text

> 李氏，蕭士綸之妻。年二十二，子生數月而寡。備嘗艱辛，苦節撫孤。知縣饒應元扁額旌表之。
> —— 康熙《溧水县志》卷之七·贞烈传

## Reading

The item is short, in three layers.

"Madam Li, wife of Xiao Shilun" gives her natal surname and her husband's name. Her given name, courtesy name, residence, and paternal family are all unrecorded, as are her husband's courtesy name and status.

"At twenty-two, a few months after her son's birth, she was widowed" gives her age and her child's infancy: widowed at twenty-two, a few months after giving birth. This is the gazetteer's sequence; her son's name and her husband's years are unrecorded.

"She endured great hardship and kept her chastity while raising the orphan" is the gazetteer's summary: enduring hardship, keeping chastity and raising the orphan (her child). The number of years of her chastity is unrecorded.

"The magistrate Rao Yingyuan honoured her with a plaque" gives the honouring official and its form. The name and the plaque form are recorded, but the plaque text is unrecorded, and this entry supplies nothing.

## The suspect heading "Sojourners"

The passage this item belongs to carries a heading "Sojourners" in the transcription. This library judges the word a suspect heading: what follows (Cheng Tianmin's daughter, the Chen daughter, Wu Ai, Madam Ma, and others) are still chaste widows and martyred women, and does not match the sense of "sojourners". This library keeps the word as recorded and marks it a suspect heading, and does not read this item as belonging to a sojourner biography; whether the passage continues the Chaste and Martyred Women biography or is another biography must be settled against the printed edition.

## Relation to Lishui

This entry enters her as "active in Lishui", and the reason must be stated. What the gazetteer records is her chastity in Lishui: wife of Xiao Shilun, widowed at twenty-two and keeping chastity while raising an orphan, finally honoured with a plaque by the county magistrate Rao Yingyuan — her community her home and the honouring official a Lishui officer. But the gazetteer records no birthplace or native place for her, naming her only "Madam Li", so this entry does not record her as "born locally". There is no office, recommendation, or writing to attach to her, so "official service", "recommended from Lishui", and "writings" do not apply; since the record is of keeping her home, not of lodging abroad, "sojourn" does not apply either. Of the five categories only "active in Lishui" holds, and this entry uses it.

## Points of doubt

First, her birth and death years are unrecorded. The gazetteer gives only that she was widowed at twenty-two; it gives no year of birth, no year for her husband, no number of years of chastity, and no year of death, and this entry converts and infers nothing.

Second, her birthplace and native place are not explicitly recorded. The gazetteer calls her only "Madam Li"; her paternal family and residence are unrecorded.

Third, beyond her husband's name, her paternal family and offspring are not fully recorded. The gazetteer gives only her husband Xiao Shilun; his courtesy name and status are unrecorded, as are the orphan's name and later career.

Fourth, only the honouring official and the plaque form are recorded, not the plaque text or the year of the honour. The gazetteer says only that the magistrate Rao Yingyuan honoured her with a plaque.

Fifth, the item is brief, with no detail beyond her widowhood, her chastity, and the honour, and this entry does not draw on other items to fill it out.

Sixth, this entry rests on a single item in this gazetteer, in the "Sojourners" (suspect heading) block of the Chaste and Martyred Women biography; the volume is an online transcription, not checked against a punctuated edition or the printed text, so citations must be marked as from the transcription.

Seventh, searches across the other source layers of this library produced nothing that could add to her.
