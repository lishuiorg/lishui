Yang Yueyin is a Luoshan Great Dragon bearer this library reached in both documents that call themselves the third batch. The two rosters are of different scope, so this entry writes what each document records separately, does not merge them into one roster, and supplies no life story.

## List status

The notice of the General Office of the Lishui District People's Government publishing the third batch of district-level representative intangible cultural heritage items and bearer rosters (Li Zheng Ban Fa [2021] No. 3) has an annex 2 headed "third batch of district-level intangible cultural heritage representative bearer roster (9 in total)". Its columns are "no. | bearer | year and month of birth | item name | item category | unit or address". This library read all nine rows, and Yang Yueyin is row 9, transcribed as written:

> 9｜杨月银｜1976年6月｜骆山大龙｜传统舞蹈｜和凤镇骆山社区

The year and month of birth and the unit or address are kept exactly as written: no age is computed from them, and the community name is not rewritten.

The notice of the Lishui District People's Government publishing the third batch of district-level protected sites, intangible cultural heritage projects and representative transmitters (Li Zheng Fa [2017] No. 197) has an annex 3 headed "list of representative transmitters (19 in total)". This library read 14 names in it, and Yang Yueyin appears in one of the rows read, which places three names under a single item name:

> 杨海洲、杨小照、杨月银（骆山大龙）

That annex gives only the names and the item name, not a year and month of birth and not an address.

## The item listed under

Row 9 of annex 2 of the 2021 document gives the item name Luoshan Great Dragon and the item category traditional dance, both as written in the annex columns; annex 3 of the 2017 document gives the item name only and no category. This entry writes these names only. The history of the item, the shape of the dragon, the origin legend and its national-level status belong to the Luoshan Great Dragon entry on the culture site (`ls:item:luoshan-dalong`) and are not repeated here.

## Relation to Lishui

Annex 2 of the 2021 document supplies the "unit or address" column, written as Luoshan Community, Hefeng Town; annex 3 of the 2017 document supplies no unit or address, only names and an item name. Both rosters are district-level rosters of Lishui, published respectively by the General Office of the Lishui District People's Government and the Lishui District People's Government, and what a bearer roster evidences is transmitting activity in Lishui — here in Luoshan Community of Hefeng Town — not place of birth, so this entry's `relation` is "activity". The village where the dragon dance is transmitted has its own entry on the towns site (`ls:place:luoshan-village`).

## Points of doubt

First, both documents call themselves the third batch while their rosters differ: annex 2 of the 2021 document claims 9 bearers and this library read all of them, annex 3 of the 2017 document claims 19 and this library read only 14. This library does not decide which roster is the official one, writes what each document records separately, and does not merge the counts.

Second, annex 3 of the 2017 document claims 19 bearers while this library read 14; the names it did not read are neither written nor supplied.

Third, annex 3 of the 2017 document places Yang Yueyin on one row with Yang Haizhou and Yang Xiaozhao under the single item name Luoshan Great Dragon. What each of the three transmits, whether they come from one line of transmission, and how the three are related are not recorded, and this entry does not guess.

Fourth, the record goes no further than a year and month of birth, and no age is computed from it; annex 3 of the 2017 document gives no year and month of birth and no address at all. Sex, native place, place of birth, teachers and lineage, life events and present situation are recorded in none of the sources this library holds; sex in particular must not be inferred from the characters of a name.

Fifth, the rosters give an item name and an item category only. What part the bearer takes in the dragon dance, how the transmission is carried out, and to whom it is transmitted are not recorded. The standing of the item itself in the national and district lists, its batch and its safeguarding unit belong to the item entry, not to this one.
