## Overview

The Fujiabian Hills are the hilly land around Fujiabian in Honglan Subdistrict, Lishui District. The district government's administrative divisions page records that Honglan Subdistrict has a residents' committee named Fujiabian, written there in abbreviated form, and the hills described in this entry take that place name as their extent.

Elevation and basin: no official document gives the elevation or the basin of these hills, so the entry declares elevation_m in gaps and records the basin as undetermined. The district government's geographical position page and its two contingency plans give district-wide figures only, namely low hills at 72.5 per cent of the district and a highest elevation of 368.5 m, and none of them names the Fujiabian area; this site does not infer from them.

The attraction: the Nanjing Municipal Bureau of Culture and Tourism's list of national graded tourist attractions in Nanjing, current to August 2026, records the Fujiabian Agricultural Science and Technology Sightseeing Park as a AAA-rated tourist attraction, at the Fujiabian Agricultural Science and Technology Park in Honglan Subdistrict, on the grading notice announced by the National Tourist Attraction Quality Grade Assessment Committee as Announcement No. 1 of 2006. The list gives the grade and the notice number but not the date of assessment, so the entry records 2006 as the year of the notice. The park's name follows the list.

## Formation and change

The Fujiabian area lies in the Ningzhenyang hill country. The district's land spatial plan near-term implementation plan records that Lishui falls from south-east to north-west, that low hills cover 72.5 per cent of the district, and that the landform of hills, ridges, slopes and valley floors interlocks in three steps; Honglan Subdistrict lies south of the district seat, and the hills around Fujiabian belong to that setting.

No first-tier source has been found for the age, rock, soil type or structural setting of these hills, so nothing is written here; nor has any source been found on their change through small-watershed management or the return of farmland to forest.

## Landscape and visiting

The Fujiabian Agricultural Science and Technology Sightseeing Park is a AAA-rated tourist attraction, on a grading notice of 2006, and its plum garden and strawberry bases are the main sights.

China Nanjing International Plum Blossom Festival: the festival is organised by the Nanjing Municipal People's Government, was first held in 1996, and in 2024 was in its twenty-ninth edition; it ran from 19 February to 15 March 2024, with Plum Blossom Mountain as its main venue and Xuanwu Lake Park and Fujiabian in Lishui as sub-venues (Nanjing Municipal People's Government website, 19 February 2024; Jiangsu Provincial Department of Culture and Tourism website, 19 February 2024). This entry records only the sub-venue arrangement of that edition; the arrangement in 2025 and later years must be checked year by year.

Lishui Plum Blossom Season: on 21 February 2026 the 2026 Lishui Plum Blossom Season opened at Fujiabian in Honglan Subdistrict, with a plum market and the lighting of the ten-li plum grove at night; more than seventy stalls were set up on the opening day and about sixty thousand visitors entered the grounds (Lishui District People's Government website, publicity office of Honglan Subdistrict, 24 February 2026). Those figures are for the opening day and must not be written as figures for the whole season.

Strawberries: on 23 February 2025 a strawberry promotion event for 2025 opened at the plaza of the Fujiabian strawberry garden (Lishui District People's Government website, Lishui District Media Centre, 21 February 2025).

In its 2025 work reply the district culture and tourism bureau listed the Sweet Picking Route, centred on Fujiabian and Shitouzhai, among seven fine holiday routes through the beautiful countryside (Lishui District People's Government, 1 December 2025); that document gives only the route name and its core attractions, not the start and finish or the distance.

No first-tier source has been found for walking routes, viewing facilities, tickets or opening times in the Fujiabian Hills, so no visiting detail is given; readers who need it should go to the district culture and tourism department, the Honglan Subdistrict authorities and the park. Opening information is current to September 2026.
