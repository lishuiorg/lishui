## Route overview

The Flower-viewing Route is one of the seven boutique holiday routes of beautiful countryside listed by the Lishui District Culture and Tourism Bureau in a work reply, built around the Rose Garden and Menghua Garden (People's Government of Lishui District, 1 December 2025). The wording of the reply is "gradually explored out"; that is the working position of the district culture and tourism bureau, not a formally issued list of routes.

The reply gives only the name of the route and what it is built around, with no start or finish, distance, intermediate points or mode of travel. This entry therefore leaves the route length, the route points and the route mode fields empty, filling them neither from that document nor by working them out from other material.

This entry does not fill the basin field: the sources available give no basin for either core feature, so none is inferred from them.

Neither core feature has an entry in this library yet, and this entry records only the names and the flower-viewing clues given officially, adding no information about the places themselves. Further, the plum festival report carried on the Nanjing Municipal People's Government website lists a flower-viewing resort named Sanye Menghua Garden, a name not identical with the Menghua Garden of the reply; as no corroboration has been found that the two are one place, this entry sets down both spellings as they stand and does not merge them.

Related entries in this library:

- Lishui Plum Blossom Season (`ls:article:lishui-shangmei-season`): the plum-viewing event organised by Lishui District itself, related to the flower-viewing theme; the two have entries of their own and do not transcribe each other.

## Along the way

- Rose Garden: one of the two core features named in the official reply. The reply gives no location, area, varieties or opening information; this entry records the name only, and the rest is to be taken from the official notices of the local subdistricts and towns and of the operators. This library has no Rose Garden entry yet.
- Menghua Garden: the other core feature named in the official reply. The report on the 2024 China Nanjing International Plum Blossom Festival carried on the Nanjing Municipal People's Government website lists a flower-viewing resort named Sanye Menghua Garden; the list gives names only, with no location or scale. This entry records that name clue only and does not on that basis assert that the two names refer to one place. This library has no Menghua Garden entry yet.
- Plum-viewing through lines and custom routes: the 2024 China Nanjing International Plum Blossom Festival had Plum Blossom Mountain as its main venue and Xuanwu Lake Park and Fujiabian in Lishui as its sub-venues; on top of the regular plum-viewing through lines to Plum Blossom Mountain and Fujiabian in Lishui, Nanjing sorted out fourteen fine plum-viewing places in the city and designed six dedicated custom plum-viewing routes (Nanjing Municipal People's Government website, 19 February 2024; Jiangsu Provincial Department of Culture and Tourism website, 19 February 2024). Neither document gives the start and finish, distance or running times of the through lines, and the municipal government document does not list the six custom routes and fourteen places one by one. This entry records those facts only and does not on that basis rewrite the fields of this route.

This entry does not copy the text of the related entries; the dates and open questions of each place are as given in its own source and entry.

## Practical information

- Mode, start and finish, distance and intermediate points: not given in the official reply, left empty here and not guessed at.
- Season: flower viewing is the main experience along the way. Plum blossom and rose flowering times differ from each other and shift from year to year; before travelling, readers should go to the official notices of the district culture and tourism department, the local subdistricts and towns and the several gardens.
