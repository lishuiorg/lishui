## Route overview

Chasing the Waves on the Lake is one of the two rural self-drive routes in Lishui recommended by the Nanjing Culture and Tourism Information Service Platform in July 2026 (Information Centre for Culture and Tourism of Nanjing, 20 July 2026). The piece is promotional writing from a municipal culture and tourism information platform, not a published document; the day-by-day itinerary and the businesses it recommends change with the season and with the businesses themselves, so the confidence of this entry does not exceed medium.

The piece gives no start or finish and no total distance, so this entry leaves the route length field empty, filling it neither from that piece nor by writing anything such as a total length for a cycling road round Shijiu Lake.

The mode is self-driving, that is the value self-driving for route mode; cycling is also part of the experience along the way. The basin is recorded as Shijiu Lake, because the route is built around Shijiu Lake, and Shijiu Lake is itself one of the district's three catchments (Lishui District People's Government website, geographical position page).

The core feature of the route, Shijiu Lake, has an entry in this library (`ls:place:shijiu-lake`). Route one in the same piece, "Fruit and Mountain Living, Songs of the Fields", is built around Fujiabian and Wuxiang Mountain and stands as a route of its own; this entry does not merge it in and does not count the two together.

## Along the way

The day-by-day itinerary is given as in the piece of July 2026, with the names of the stops as they stand:

- Day one: the Mirror of the Sky at Shijiu Lake, then cycling on the lakeside road, passing a lighthouse, the Zhangxu pier and the Zhujia love tree, then the Huize Water Sports Base, then the sunset viewpoint over Shijiu Lake. "Mirror of the Sky" is a name used in the media and in circulation, not a geographical name (according to People's Daily Online, 10 November 2020); this entry sets it down as a name only and does not list it alongside geographical facts.
- Shijiu Lake: the boundary lake in southern Lishui District, with an entry of its own in this library, where the two versions of its area, its designations and its open questions are given. This entry points out only that the route is built around it and does not copy its text.
- Day two: picking at Fuhua, then Luoshan Fireworks Camping Park (barbecue, open-air cinema, fireworks and tented accommodation). The name of the park contains "Luoshan", the same as Luoshan Village in Hefeng Town; whether the two are one place is not stated in the piece, so this site does not assert it, nor write the park as a facility of Luoshan Village.

This entry does not copy the text of the related entries; the scale, the designations and the open questions of each place are as given in its own source and entry.

## Practical information

- Mode: self-driving, with cycling also part of the experience along the way.
- Start and finish and distance: not given in the recommendation, left empty here and not guessed at.
- Date: the itinerary is the summer version of July 2026, and the day-by-day arrangements, the businesses recommended and the activities change; opening times, charges and safety requirements for camping, water sports and fireworks are to be taken from the official notices of the operators and of the local authorities.
- Seasonal difference: swans winter at Shijiu Lake, which is not the same season as the summer water-based experiences; see the Birdwatching and Camping at Shijiu Lake entry in this library.
- Information date: this entry was checked to September 2026.
