Putang Bridge is a Ming dynasty stone arch bridge over the Putang River in what is now Honglan Subdistrict, Lishui District. It was listed in 2013 in the seventh batch of Major Historical and Cultural Sites Protected at the National Level. A People's Daily report of 2015 noted that of the many old bridges in the Nanjing area, only two are national-level protected sites, and Putang Bridge is one of them.
