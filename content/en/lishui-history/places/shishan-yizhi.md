The Shishan Site lies at what is now Shishan, Shiqiu Subdistrict, Lishui District. In the list attached to the district government's notice on the third batch of district-level protected sites (Li Zheng Fa [2017] No. 197) the site is recorded as Neolithic, and its location under the name then current, Shishan, Shiqiu Town; Shiqiu Town became Shiqiu Subdistrict in 2018, and `address` uses the current name.

The district list gives only the period, the level and the location. It gives neither the extent, the area nor the stratigraphy of the site, and neither an excavation report nor a survey report for it has been traced. This entry therefore describes neither the remains nor the finds, and `era` reproduces the period recorded in the list.
