Jinjing Spring stands at what is now Baishiguan Village, Jingqiao Town, Lishui District. In the list attached to the district government's notice on the third batch of district-level protected sites (Li Zheng Fa [2017] No. 197) it is recorded as Qing, at Baishiguan Village, Jingqiao Town.

The list gives only the period, the level and the location. It gives neither the form of the well nor the construction of its kerb, nor its depth or the state of its water, and it does not say whether "Jinjing" and "yongquan" are two names for one feature or two features standing together. This entry therefore describes neither the spring nor the well, and `era` reproduces the period recorded in the list.
