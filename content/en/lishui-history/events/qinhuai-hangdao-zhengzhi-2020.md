## The works and the date of acceptance

According to a notice from the Jiangsu Provincial Department of Transport, on 29 December 2020 the Honglan lock works and six bridge works, including Maojiacun Bridge, on the Qinhuai River (Shijiu Lake in Lishui–Pengfu in Jiangning) waterway regulation project passed completion inspection.

## The extent of the contract section

"Shijiu Lake in Lishui–Pengfu in Jiangning" is the name of the contract section and follows the wording of the notice. The section runs from Shijiu Lake in Lishui District to Pengfu in Jiangning, and the part in Lishui consists of the Honglan lock and the bridges along the line. This entry gives the name of the project, the extent of the section and the date of acceptance only; the cost, the waterway class and the navigation standard are not given by the source and are not supplied here.

## Uncertainties

The position, dimensions and capacity of the Honglan lock, and the names of the six bridges (the notice names only Maojiacun Bridge), are not given by the sources available here and remain to be supplied.
