## Background

In 589 CE, the ninth year of the Kaihuang era, the gazetteer records that Danyang Commandery was abolished and Jiangzhou established. In the fourth year of the Daye era Jiangzhou was in turn made into Danyang Commandery, so that the two names exchanged places and the authority above Lishui County changed.

## Time and place

The gazetteer records:

> 炀帝大业四年戊辰，以蒋州置丹阳郡，溧水隶之。
>
> *Translated:* In the fourth year of the Daye era of Emperor Yang, a *wuchen* year, Jiangzhou was made into Danyang Commandery, and Lishui was placed under it.

The fourth year of the Daye era is 608 CE. Lishui County had already been established in 591 CE; the county itself was unchanged, only its superior authority.

## What happened

Jiangzhou was abolished, its territory made into Danyang Commandery, and Lishui County transferred to the commandery.

## Consequences

Lishui passed from Jiangzhou to Danyang Commandery. In 620 CE, the third year of the Wude era, Lishui and Jiangning were made into Yangzhou, with Lishui County not abolished and still under it; in 626 CE, the ninth year of Wude, Lishui was transferred to Xuanzhou.

## Uncertainties

The entry is a single sentence and gives neither the seat nor the subordinate counties of Danyang Commandery. Whether this commandery was the same unit as the one abolished in 589, and whether its extent had changed, is not stated in the sources; this entry draws no inference. The confidence level is set to `medium`.
