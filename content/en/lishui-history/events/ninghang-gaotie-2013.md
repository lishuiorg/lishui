## Opening

According to People's Daily Online, the Nanjing–Hangzhou High-Speed Railway opened on 1 July 2013. The report gives only the opening date, not the length of the Lishui section or how it was built.

## The line and the station in Lishui

According to the district government's district overview, the line crosses Lishui District and has a station at Lishui in Dongping Subdistrict; a planned high-speed line from Nanjing via Xuancheng to Huangshan will also pass through Lishui and have a station there. The site, size and opening date of Lishui station are matters for the railway authorities' announcements, and the sources available here do not give them.

## Uncertainties

Dongping Subdistrict is the current name. When the line opened the area was Dongping Town, which became a subdistrict in May 2018. This entry follows the district government's present-day wording and does not rewrite the name in use at the time.
