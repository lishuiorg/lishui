## The provincial list: batches, dates and entries

| Batch | Published | Lishui entries | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First | 7 April 2020 | Cangkou in Tangxi Village, Lixiang in Shitouzhai Village, Shishanxia in Ruijia Village, Zhujia in Zhangjia Village; four in all | List of 8 April 2020 on the provincial housing department website |
| Third | January 2021 | Putang in Putang Community, Honglan Subdistrict; one | List of 11 January 2021 on the provincial housing department website (reprinted from Ziniu News) |
| Sixth | 2023 | Tai Village, Luoshan, Yangjia Village in Sunjiaxiang and two others, five in all, of which three are named | Reply of 28 November 2023 from the district culture and tourism bureau |
| Seventh | January 2024 | Shannan Village; one | Nanjing Daily, 31 January 2024 |
| Eighth | August 2025 | Shangzhuang in Shanggang Community, Chenguo in Wuqiao Village; two | Nanjing municipal government website, 22 August 2025; notice-period report of 14 July 2025 |

The first batch named 107 villages in all, identified by the provincial department of housing and urban-rural development with the provincial departments of finance, natural resources, agriculture and rural affairs, and culture and tourism. The third batch named 138 villages, 18 of them in Nanjing, identified and published with the agreement of the Jiangsu Provincial People's Government. The eighth batch named 70 villages, five of them in Nanjing.

Three points attach to the third-batch row. First, that page sits in the "media coverage" column of the provincial housing department site and reprints a report of 7 January 2021 from Ziniu News; it is not the gazette text of the recognition decision, and it gives neither the document number nor the publication date of that decision. Second, only Putang appears for Lishui in that batch, while the district government and Nanjing Morning Post attribute other Lishui villages such as Shuijing and Shanjiaxu to other batches; this library presents both statements side by side and rewrites neither. Third, the page reports a moment in January 2021, before the seventh and eighth batches were published, so it cannot be used to compute Lishui's total.

## The national list

The sixth batch of Chinese traditional villages, published on 20 March 2023 by the Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development and other departments, includes two villages in Lishui: Shitouzhai Village in Baima Town and Zhangjia Village in Hefeng Town. Both are the administrative villages of settlements that also appear on the provincial list, Lixiang and Zhujia respectively. Note how the date is stated: October 2022 was the period of publicity, March 2023 was the formal publication. "Included" is not wrong, but the precise formulation is "formally published in March 2023". Lishui is accordingly the only Nanjing district with two villages on the national list.

## Eleven identified by name against an official twelve

A district government reply of November 2025, giving figures to the end of 2024, records that the district has 2 Chinese traditional villages and 12 provincial traditional villages. Identified one by one from the published lists, this library finds 11 provincial traditional villages by name: four in the first batch, one in the third, three in the sixth, one in the seventh and two in the eighth. The official total exceeds this by one.

The gap most probably lies in the sixth batch. That reply gives a total of five while naming only three, Tai Village, Luoshan and Yangjia Village in Sunjiaxiang, leaving two unnamed. This library has identified only Putang for the third batch, so the gap has not narrowed to a particular village. Once the two names from the sixth batch are established, 11 and 12 should reconcile. Until then both figures are presented side by side: the official 12 does not overwrite the 11, and the 11 does not deny the 12.

Note also the difference in point of time behind 11 and 12: a Nanjing Morning Post report of March 2023 gives eleven provincial traditional villages for Lishui, while the district replies of 2024 and 2025 give twelve. The difference arises from when the count was taken — the eighth batch had not yet been published in March 2023 — and is not an error. That this library's by-name result of eleven happens to equal the figure in that report is a coincidence of different bases and cannot be used to corroborate either.

## Points of doubt

- The third-batch page is a reprint of a media report on the official site of the provincial housing department; the recognition document number and publication date are not given there. Should the recognition document be obtained later, it should be recorded in a new source card rather than by amending this entry.
- The formal published document for the sixth provincial batch is not held in this library; both its names and its total rest on the district bureau's reply.
- Only Putang appears for Lishui in the third batch, while the district government and the Nanjing Morning Post name other Lishui villages such as Shuijing and Shanjiaxu; the two sets of batch attributions disagree, and this library presents them side by side without merging them.
- The seventh-batch report writes Shannan Village together with the former town name Honglan; the standard village name and its administrative village still need checking.
- Whether the official figure of 12 refers to exactly the same set as the itemised lists, for instance whether it counts the administrative villages of the Chinese traditional villages, is not explained in any document held here.
