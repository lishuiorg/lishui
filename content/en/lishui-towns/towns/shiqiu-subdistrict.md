## Overview

Shiqiu Subdistrict lies in the west of Lishui District. Its profile page gives the area as 116.5 sq km, with 14 villages and communities, of which 8 are villages and 6 communities, a resident population of 73,000 and a registered population of 51,000. Two universities stand within it, Jiangsu Second Normal University and the Pujiang College of Nanjing Tech University.

Its statistical division code is 320117004, as recorded by the Department of Civil Affairs of Jiangsu Province in its table of county-level and below changes for 2018.

## Administrative history

In May 2018 the provincial government approved the abolition of Shiqiu Town and the establishment of Shiqiu Subdistrict; the approval was issued on 15 May 2018.

## Two sets of figures for area and jurisdiction

- Area: 116.47 sq km on the administrative divisions page; "116.5 sq km" on the profile page, a rounded form.
- Jurisdiction: 6 residents' committees and 8 villagers' committees on the administrative divisions page; "14 villages and communities" in the profile page's combined wording.

The two add up to the same 14. The entry fields follow the itemised version.

## Points of doubt

- The origin of the name Shiqiu and its relation to Shiqiu Dam are not recorded in the sources held here; a place-name entry awaits a source.
- Resident and registered populations appear only on the profile page, which carries no reference date.
