## Overview

Dongping Subdistrict lies in the east of Lishui District. The administrative divisions page gives its area as 106.28 sq km, with 5 residents' committees and 5 villagers' committees; its seat is at Fangbian residents' committee.

Its statistical division code is 320117003, as recorded by the Department of Civil Affairs of Jiangsu Province in its table of county-level and below changes for 2018.

## Administrative history

In May 2018 the provincial government approved the abolition of Dongping Town and the establishment of Dongping Subdistrict, its office within Fangbian residents' committee; the approval was issued on 15 May 2018.

The approval defined the subdistrict's jurisdiction as six residents' committees, Fangbian, Xuxi, Changle, Dinghu, Jinhu and Qunli, and six villagers' committees, Ailian, Aimin, Heping, Lishan, Bailu and Jingshan. The administrative divisions page now lists five and five. The two accounts differ by Qunli residents' committee and Jingshan villagers' committee, indicating a later adjustment whose date and document are not held in this library.

## Points of doubt

- The document and date of the adjustment from six and six to five and five are not held in this library.
- The entry fields use the current administrative divisions page. Any citation of the 2018 approval must make clear that its figures describe the jurisdiction at that date.
