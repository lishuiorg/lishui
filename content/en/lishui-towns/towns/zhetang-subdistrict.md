## Overview

Zhetang Subdistrict lies in the north of Lishui District. The administrative divisions page writes the unit as "Zhetang Subdistrict (Development Zone)", sharing its administration with the Nanjing Lishui Economic Development Zone; the profiles section likewise carries a page headed "Development Zone", in keeping with that wording.

The page lists 16 residents' committees and 0 villagers' committees, making this the only one of the district's eight units with no villagers' committee.

## Administrative history

In July 2015 the provincial government approved the abolition of Zhetang Town and the establishment of Zhetang Subdistrict over the same area, its office at 8 Tuanshan West Road. The approval was issued on 15 July 2015.

## Points of doubt

- The nature of the shared administration with the development zone, and whether the subdistrict's area has followed changes to the zone, are not recorded in the sources used here.
- The area of 134.06 sq km comes from the administrative divisions page; no separate profile page for this unit is held in this library, so area and population cannot be checked against a second source.
