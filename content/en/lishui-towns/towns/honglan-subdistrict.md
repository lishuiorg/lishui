## Overview

Honglan Subdistrict lies in the south of Lishui District. The administrative divisions page gives its area as 108.99 sq km, with 8 residents' committees and 4 villagers' committees; its seat is at Sanliting residents' committee.

Its statistical division code is 320117005, as recorded by the Department of Civil Affairs of Jiangsu Province in its table of county-level and below changes for 2018.

## Administrative history

In May 2018 the provincial government approved the abolition of Honglan Town and the establishment of Honglan Subdistrict; the approval was issued on 15 May 2018. The district government's historical evolution page records only the month.

## Traditional villages within the subdistrict

This subdistrict holds more provincial traditional villages than any other in the district. Three can be identified by name:

- Cangkou, in Tangxi Village, in the first batch of traditional villages of Jiangsu (2020).
- Shannan Village, in the seventh batch (announced January 2024). The Nanjing Daily report writes "Shannan Village, Honglan Town, Lishui", reproducing the former town name; the village now belongs to Honglan Subdistrict.
- Shangzhuang, in Shanggang Community, in the eighth batch (published August 2025, publicly noticed the previous July).

## Points of doubt

- All three were identified village by village from the published lists; each has its own entry in the villages section of this library. This entry records only the relationship and does not repeat the village texts.
- In the seventh-batch report, Shannan Village is written together with the former town name; the standard village name still needs checking.
