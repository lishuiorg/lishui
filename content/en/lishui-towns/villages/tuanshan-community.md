## Overview

Tuanshan Community belongs to Zhetang Subdistrict and is the seat of the subdistrict office. The district government's administrative divisions page gives Zhetang Subdistrict (Lishui Development Zone) an area of 134.06 km² with 16 residents' committees and no villagers' committees, and records the seat as "Tuanshan Community Residents' Committee", first in the list of the sixteen.

The page sets the seat of the subdistrict office in a column of its own beside the committee names, so Tuanshan is both where the Zhetang Subdistrict office sits and one of the residents' committees under it. The boundaries, household count and population of Tuanshan Community are not recorded in the sources used here.

## Corroboration by the street address

There is a record here that corroborates. The reply Su Zheng Fu [2015] No. 69 of 15 July 2015, in the clause withdrawing Zhetang Town and establishing Zhetang Subdistrict, records "the subdistrict office seated at No. 8 Tuanshan West Road". The first part of that street name is Tuanshan, which can be checked against the administrative divisions page recording Tuanshan Community Residents' Committee as the seat: the two sources give a street address and a committee name respectively, and the street name and the committee name share their origin.

This is one of the few corroborations of a seat in this library. The reply of 2015 concerns both Yongyang and Zhetang; the Yongyang clause records "the subdistrict office seated at No. 34 Qinhuai Road", whose street name does not share an origin with the Banqiao Residents' Committee, so the two there can only be placed side by side (see the entry for Banqiao Community).

It should be noted that the reply records the street address at the time of establishment in 2015; whether the office has since moved is not recorded in the sources. The administrative divisions page names Zhetang Subdistrict and the development zone together in one row, and whether the development zone has a separate seat is likewise not recorded.

## Points of doubt

- "Community" and "residents' committee" are used interchangeably in official wording; this entry takes the community as its unit type, matching the administrative divisions page, which counts all 118 villages and communities together.
- The extent, date of establishment and origin of the name of Tuanshan Community are not recorded in the sources used here.
- "No. 8 Tuanshan West Road" is the street address recorded in the 2015 reply; whether the office has since moved, this library holds no later document.
- Whether the wording "Zhetang Subdistrict (Development Zone)" refers to two different bodies, and whether the development zone has its own seat, is not recorded in the sources.
