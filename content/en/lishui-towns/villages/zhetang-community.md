## Overview

Zhetang Community belongs to Zhetang Subdistrict and is a residents' committee. The district government's administrative divisions page gives Zhetang Subdistrict (Lishui Development Zone) 16 residents' committees and no villagers' committees, of which Zhetang is one.

## Same name, different seat

Zhetang Subdistrict takes its name from Zhetang, yet the seat of the subdistrict office is recorded as "Tuanshan Community Residents' Committee". The namesake community and the seat stand in separate columns of the administrative divisions page, showing that the subdistrict is named after Zhetang while its office sits at Tuanshan.

## Administrative history

In July 2015 the provincial government approved the abolition of Zhetang Town and the establishment of the Zhetang Subdistrict office over the same area, the office sitting at No. 8 Tuanshan West Road; the reply was issued on 15 July 2015. The subdistrict's area is that of the former town, unchanged.
