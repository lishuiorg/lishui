## Traditional village listing

In its reply of 28 November 2023 the Lishui District Culture and Tourism Bureau states that Yangjia Village in Sunjiaxiang was included, together with Tai Village in Jingqiao, Luoshan in Hefeng and two others, in the sixth batch of traditional villages of Jiangsu Province. This entry rests on the working account in that reply; the formal list is authoritative as published by the provincial housing department.

## Points of doubt

- The formally published list is not held in this library; the form "Yangjia Village, Sunjiaxiang" is reproduced as it appears in the reply, and whether it matches the standard village name still needs checking.
- Yangjia Village belongs to Sunjiaxiang Village, Hefeng Town. The early history of the village, its surviving buildings and the origin of its name are not recorded in the sources used here.
