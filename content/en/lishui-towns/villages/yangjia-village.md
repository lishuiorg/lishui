## Traditional village listing

In its reply of 28 November 2023 the Lishui District Culture and Tourism Bureau records that Sunjiaxiang Yangjia Village entered the sixth batch of traditional villages of Jiangsu together with Tai Village in Jingqiao, Luoshan and two others, five villages in all. This entry rests on the working account in that reply; the formal list is to be read from the document published by the provincial housing department.

## The reply's form against the divisions page

The reply writes the name as Sunjiaxiang Yangjia Village, a three-level compound of administrative village and settlement. The district government's administrative divisions page records that Hefeng Town has 6 villagers' committees, one of which is Sunjiaxiang, while Yangjia does not appear among the names of those 6. Read together, Sunjiaxiang is the administrative village and the traditional village entered on the list is the settlement of Yangjia inside it.

This differs from Tai Village in Jingqiao in the same reply: Tai Village is both the name of a villagers' committee of Jingqiao Town and a name on the list in its own right, whereas Yangjia is a settlement inside Sunjiaxiang Village and is not a separate committee. Hence this entry and the entry for Sunjiaxiang are separate.

## Points of doubt

- The formal document for the sixth batch is not held in this library. The reply's form Sunjiaxiang Yangjia Village is reproduced as written; it is not the same level as Sunjiaxiang on the administrative divisions page, and the standard form of the village name still needs checking.
- The date of founding, surviving structures and the origin of the name of Yangjia Village are not recorded in the sources used here.
- The reply names only three villages while giving a total of five, leaving two unnamed; the list must not be completed on this basis.
