## Overview

Tangxi Village belongs to Honglan Subdistrict and is a villagers' committee. The district government's administrative divisions page gives Honglan Subdistrict four villagers' committees — Tangxi, Qingfeng, Chenbian and Jiangjia — with Tangxi listed first.

## Traditional villages within it

The list of the first batch of traditional villages of Jiangsu, carried on the provincial housing department's website on 8 April 2020, records Cangkou as "Cangkou, Tangxi Village, Honglan Subdistrict" — a three-level form of subdistrict, administrative village and settlement. Cangkou entered the list in April 2020 and retains three ancestral halls, of the Rui, Fan and Qiu families.

## Points of doubt

- The boundaries, household count, population and origin of the name of Tangxi Village itself are not recorded in the sources used here; this entry records only its relation as the administrative village to which Cangkou belongs.
- Cangkou and Tangxi Village have separate entries: Cangkou as a settlement, this one as the administrative village.
