## Overview

Cangkou Village belongs to Tangxi Village, Honglan Subdistrict. The list published on the provincial housing department's website on 8 April 2020 records 107 villages in the first batch of traditional villages of Jiangsu, four of them in Lishui District; Cangkou is one, written as "Cangkou, Tangxi Village, Honglan Subdistrict".

## Traditional village listing

The first batch of traditional villages of Jiangsu was identified by the provincial department of housing and urban-rural development together with the provincial departments of finance, natural resources, agriculture and rural affairs, and culture and tourism, and published on 7 April 2020.

## Surnames and ancestral halls

The Yangtse Evening Post reports that the village retains "a distinctive octagonal pavilion, the century-old ancestral halls of the Rui, Fan and Qiu families, and Ming-dynasty canal transport remains". The report carries no publication date and is used here only as evidence of existence: the ancestral halls belong chiefly to the Rui, Fan and Qiu families, and no date or protection grade is inferred for any of them. The form, grade and publication batch of the buildings belong to the history site of this group; this entry records only village-level facts.

## Points of doubt

- The Yangtse Evening Post report carries no date, and "century-old" is the report's own wording, not a dating.
- The date of the village's founding and its early history are not recorded in the sources used here.
- Cangkou is a settlement within Tangxi Village and has a separate entry from the administrative village.
