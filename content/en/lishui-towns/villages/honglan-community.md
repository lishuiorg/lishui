## Overview

Honglan Community belongs to Honglan Subdistrict and is a residents' committee. The district government's administrative divisions page records that Honglan Subdistrict has 8 residents' committees and 4 villagers' committees, with Honglan first among the 8. The name column on that page gives only the two characters Honglan, without the general term for community, which this entry supplies following official usage.

The 8 residents' committees are Honglan, Sanliting, Tianshengqiao, Xiwang, Wuxiangsi, Fujiabian, Putang and Shanggang; the 4 villagers' committees are Tangxi, Qingfeng, Chenbian and Jiangjia. Comparing the subtotals for the eight towns and subdistricts on the same page, Yongyang Subdistrict with 29 and Zhetang Subdistrict with 16 both exceed Honglan, placing its 8 third.

## Same name, different seat

Honglan Subdistrict takes its name from Honglan, yet the seat of the subdistrict office is recorded as "Sanliting Residents' Committee". The namesake community and the seat stand in separate columns of the administrative divisions page, showing that the subdistrict is named after Honglan while its office sits at Sanliting.

This arrangement is consistent with the 2018 reply: the clause abolishing Honglan Town and establishing Honglan Subdistrict names no seat of its own, and today's administrative divisions page gives the seat as Sanliting. Neither source makes Honglan Community the seat.

## Administrative history

In May 2018 the provincial government approved the abolition of Honglan Town and the establishment of Honglan Subdistrict; it was issued on 15 May 2018. The district government's history of administrative changes page records only the year and month.

## Points of doubt

- The extent, household count, population and origin of the name of Honglan Community are not recorded in the sources used here, nor is the process by which Honglan came by its name.
- This library holds no reply that lists the 8 residents' committees and 4 villagers' committees of Honglan Subdistrict one by one, so the differences before and after 2018 cannot be checked name by name. This differs from Dongping Subdistrict, for which the reply does give a full list.
