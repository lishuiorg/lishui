## Overview

Honglan Community belongs to Honglan Subdistrict and is a residents' committee. The district government's administrative divisions page gives Honglan Subdistrict 8 residents' committees and 4 villagers' committees, with Honglan first among the eight.

## Same name, different seat

Honglan Subdistrict takes its name from Honglan, yet the seat of the subdistrict office is recorded as "Sanliting Residents' Committee". The namesake community and the seat stand in separate columns of the administrative divisions page, showing that the subdistrict is named after Honglan while its office sits at Sanliting.

## Administrative history

In May 2018 the provincial government approved the abolition of Honglan Town and the establishment of Honglan Subdistrict; the reply was issued on 15 May 2018. The district government's historical evolution page gives only the year and month.

## Points of doubt

- The extent, household count, population and origin of the name of Honglan Community are not recorded in the sources used here, and neither is the origin of the name Honglan itself.
