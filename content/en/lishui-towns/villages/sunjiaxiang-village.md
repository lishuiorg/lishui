## Overview

Sunjiaxiang Village belongs to Hefeng Town and is a villagers' committee. The district government's administrative divisions page gives Hefeng Town six villagers' committees, with Sunjiaxiang first in the list.

## Traditional villages within it

A reply to a district CPPCC proposal by the district culture and tourism bureau on 28 November 2023 records "Taicun in Jingqiao, Luoshan in Hefeng and Yangjia in Sunjiaxiang, among five villages listed in the sixth batch of traditional villages of Jiangsu". The sentence writes Yangjia as "Yangjia in Sunjiaxiang", joining administrative village and settlement, which shows Yangjia to be a settlement within Sunjiaxiang.

The sentence names only three of the five and gives the total, leaving the other two names unrecorded.

## Village area

The size of Sunjiaxiang Village can be recorded from a further government source. According to the presentation of the results of the Conservation and Development Plan for the Yangjia Traditional Village, Sunjiaxiang Village, Hefeng Town, issued on 7 September 2026 by the Lishui Branch of the Nanjing Municipal Bureau of Planning and Natural Resources, the study range of the plan extends to the administrative village of Sunjiaxiang, with a village area of 599.03 hectares, while the plan extent is the boundary of the natural village of Yangjia in Sunjiaxiang Village, about 12.72 hectares; the plan was approved by the Nanjing municipal government on 17 August 2026.

The two figures are of different levels: 599.03 hectares is the area of the administrative village and 12.72 hectares that of the natural village of Yangjia inside it, and the two must not be used interchangeably. The figure is that of the conservation plan; this library holds neither the full plan text nor its drawings, so no parcel numbers or boundary coordinates are entered from it, and it is not converted to the figures of the administrative divisions page.

## Points of doubt

- The reply gives a total of five villages in the sixth batch but names only three; the other two names are not recorded in the sources used here.
- The boundaries, household count, population and origin of the name of Sunjiaxiang Village itself are not recorded in the sources used here; the 599.03 hectares quoted above is a figure of the conservation plan.
- Neither the full text nor the drawings of the plan are held in this library.
