## Overview

Shiqiu Community belongs to Shiqiu Subdistrict and is the seat of the subdistrict office. The district government's administrative divisions page gives Shiqiu Subdistrict an area of 116.47 km² with 6 residents' committees and 8 villagers' committees, and records the seat as "Shiqiu Residents' Committee". The subdistrict profile page counts the same units together as "14 villages and communities, of which 8 villages and 6 communities", agreeing with the itemised table.

The six residents' committees are Shiqiu, Tangdoucun, Mingjue, Guangming, Xietang and Shedong; the eight villagers' committees are Dongquan, Xiangyang, Sanxing, Tongxin, Sangyuanpu, Hengshan, Jiutang and Shangfang. Tangdoucun ends in the character for "village" yet is a residents' committee; the generic suffix does not match its actual tier, and this library records the official wording as it stands. The boundaries, household count and population of Shiqiu Community are not recorded in the sources used here.

## Points of doubt

- The residents' committee named Tangdoucun carries the character for "village" while several villagers' committees carry no suffix at all; the inconsistent wording is the official table's own and has not been rewritten.
- The extent, date of establishment and origin of the name of Shiqiu Community are not recorded in the sources used here.
