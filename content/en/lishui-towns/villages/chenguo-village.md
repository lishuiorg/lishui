## Traditional village listing

A notice published on the Nanjing municipal government website on 22 August 2025 records that the eighth batch of traditional villages of Jiangsu named 70 villages, five of them in Nanjing, among them "Chenguo, Wuqiao Village, Hefeng Town". The report of 14 July 2025 from the district media centre concerns the public notice period for the same selection; the formal list is authoritative as published by the provincial housing department.

## Points of doubt

- The date of founding, the origin of the name and surviving structures are not recorded in the sources used here.
- Chenguo is a settlement within Wuqiao Village and has a separate entry from the administrative village.
