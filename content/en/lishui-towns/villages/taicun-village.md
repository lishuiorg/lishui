## Traditional village listing

In its reply of 28 November 2023 the Lishui District Culture and Tourism Bureau states: "Tai Village in Jingqiao, Luoshan in Hefeng, Yangjia Village in Sunjiaxiang and two others, five villages in all, entered the sixth batch of the traditional village list of Jiangsu." This entry rests on the working account in that reply; the formal document for the sixth batch is not held in this library.

## Read against the current divisions

The district government's administrative divisions page records that Jingqiao Town has 5 residents' committees and 7 villagers' committees, the latter being Shuijing, Dacun, Zhishan, Yunheshan, Kongjia, Xiantan and Tai Village, with Tai Village last of the seven. This agrees with the account of November 2023: Tai Village is both an administrative unit of Jingqiao Town and a traditional village entered in the sixth batch in its own name, with the two sources using the same form of the name and no variant appearing.

The reply names only three of the five villages, so the other two remain unnamed and the list cannot be completed from it.

## Points of doubt

- The reply names only three villages while giving a total of five, leaving two unnamed; the list must not be completed on this basis.
- The date of founding, the origin of the name and surviving structures are not recorded in the sources used here.
- The formal document for the sixth batch is not held in this library, so the batch year rests on the timing of the reply of November 2023.
