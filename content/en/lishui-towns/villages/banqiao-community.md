## Overview

Banqiao Community belongs to Yongyang Subdistrict and is the seat of the subdistrict office. The district government's administrative divisions page gives Yongyang Subdistrict an area of 130.31 km² with 29 residents' committees and 3 villagers' committees, and records the seat as "Banqiao Residents' Committee". The three villagers' committees are Dongshan, Gaotang and Qiuhu.

The Yongyang Subdistrict profile page counts the 29 residents' committees and 3 villagers' committees together as "32 communities", a different presentation from the itemised table but the same figure. The boundaries, household count and population of Banqiao Community are not recorded in the sources used here.

## Points of doubt

- Banqiao is a common place name around Nanjing; the subject here is Banqiao Community in Yongyang Subdistrict, Lishui District, and has nothing to do with Banqiao Subdistrict in Yuhuatai District.
- The extent, date of establishment and origin of the name of Banqiao Community are not recorded in the sources used here.
