## Overview

Heping Village belongs to Dongping Subdistrict. The district government's administrative divisions page lists the subdistrict's five villagers' committees as Ailian, Aimin, Heping, Lishan and Bailu, with Heping third.

## Administrative history

In May 2018 the provincial government approved the abolition of Dongping Town and the establishment of Dongping Subdistrict; the reply includes Heping Villagers' Committee within the new subdistrict's area. It was issued on 15 May 2018.
