## Overview

Xinqiao Community belongs to Jingqiao Town and is the seat of the town people's government. The district government's administrative divisions page gives Jingqiao Town an area of 134.52 km² with 5 residents' committees and 7 villagers' committees, and records the seat as "Xinqiao Residents' Committee".

The five residents' committees are Taocun, Hangcun, Xinqiao, Fengxiangling and Ruijia; the seven villagers' committees are Shuijing, Dacun, Zhishan, Yunheshan, Kongjia, Xiantan and Taicun. Ruijia is a residents' committee and contains the provincial traditional village of Shishanxia; Taicun is a villagers' committee and is itself a provincial traditional village. The seat does not share the town's name. The boundaries, household count and population of Xinqiao Community are not recorded in the sources used here.

## Points of doubt

- The residents' committees of Jingqiao Town — Taocun, Hangcun and Ruijia — end in or consist of village names, yet their tier is that of residents' committees; this library records the official wording without rewriting the suffix.
- The extent, date of establishment and origin of the name of Xinqiao Community are not recorded in the sources used here.
