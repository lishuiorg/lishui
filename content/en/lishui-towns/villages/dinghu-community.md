## Overview

Dinghu Community belongs to Dongping Subdistrict. The district government's administrative divisions page lists the subdistrict's five residents' committees as Fangbian, Xuxi, Changle, Dinghu and Jinhu, with Dinghu fourth.

## Administrative history

In May 2018 the provincial government approved the abolition of Dongping Town and the establishment of Dongping Subdistrict; the reply includes Dinghu Residents' Committee within the new subdistrict's area. It was issued on 15 May 2018.
