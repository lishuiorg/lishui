## Traditional village listing

The Nanjing Daily reported on 31 January 2024 that the seventh batch of provincial traditional villages had been published, with three new villages in Nanjing, among them "Shannan Village, Honglan Town, Lishui", bringing the city's total to 65. The report's "Honglan Town" reproduces the former town name; Honglan Town was reconstituted as Honglan Subdistrict in May 2018.

## Points of doubt

- The report gives only the town and village names and does not record the administrative village Shannan belongs to, its date of founding or its surviving structures.
- The standard form of the name "Shannan Village" and its administrative village still need checking.
