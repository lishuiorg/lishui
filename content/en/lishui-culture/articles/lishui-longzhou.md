## The fourth Honglan dragon-boat race, 2026

According to a report by the district media centre, the fourth Lishui Honglan dragon-boat race, sponsored under the name Nongshanghang Cup, was held in 2026, with 27 dragon-boat teams taking part. The report says the event drew 200,000 onlookers counted together on site and online.

The figure of 200,000 is the report's combined total and includes online viewing; it is not the number of spectators on site. The qualifier "on site and online" is retained here.

## Uncertainties

First, the edition number and the sponsor's name are reproduced as they stand in the report and are not abbreviated; the level of the event and its organisers should follow the official documents of the race, and this page is only a lead.

Second, no material on other years of the race is held by this site, and its history and earlier scale are not supplied here.
