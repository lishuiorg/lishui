## Name and category

According to the public notice of January 2024, Black Rice Festival is one of the new inscriptions in the recommended list for the fourth batch of district-level intangible cultural heritage items, in the folk-custom category. Two folk-custom items were submitted by the Hefeng Town People's Government in this batch; the other is Horn Lantern.

## Applicant and place of transmission

The applicant unit named in the notice is the Hefeng Town People's Government. The first part of the title is the name of a seasonal food, and the word rendered here as "festival" presumably refers to a gathering or a temple procession; the notice gives no date, no site and no list of participating villages.

## Wording of the item name

The notice uses this three-character name and not the names of the food itself or of a black-rice festival day. This entry follows the wording of the notice and does not merge the item with other names.

## To be added

## Points in doubt

This library holds no source on the seasonal date associated with black rice, so no date is given here; the point awaits a source.
