## Name and category

According to the public notice of January 2024, Jinling Qipao Making is one of the new inscriptions in the recommended list for the fourth batch of district-level intangible cultural heritage items, in the traditional craft category.

## Applicant

The applicant unit named in the notice is the Lishui Development Zone administrative committee. Most of the 17 projects recommended in this batch were submitted by town people's governments or subdistrict offices; this is the only one submitted by the development zone committee.

## Bearer

The same notice lists Tao Yumei as a bearer in this batch, for Jinling Qipao Making.

## To be added

- the individual steps of measuring, pattern-making, cutting, edging and knot-button making;
- the choice of outer and lining fabrics;
- the relation between the styles made and local dress customs;
- the workshop or teaching premises, the line of transmission and the safeguarding measures.

## Points in doubt

The first part of the item name is a historical place name for the Nanjing region. The notice does not say when or by what route the craft reached Lishui, and this entry draws no inference.
