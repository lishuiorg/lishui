According to the public notice of January 2024, Shangfang Sweet Potato Vermicelli Making is one of the new inscriptions in the recommended list for the fourth batch of district-level intangible cultural heritage items. Its category is traditional craft and its applicant unit is the Shiqiu Subdistrict Office. The first part of the title corresponds to Shangfang Village in Shiqiu Subdistrict; the second part uses the local word for sweet potato, which the notice writes in that form and not with the alternative words for the crop.

> **Entry to be expanded.** This is a skeleton entry: at present it guarantees only that the fields and sources are complete. Still to be added: the sweet-potato varieties used and the area they come from, the extraction of the starch and the preparation of the batter, the extrusion and drying of the noodles, their form and how they are cooked, the villages of transmission and the line of transmission.
> Until then, no unverified details will be entered.
