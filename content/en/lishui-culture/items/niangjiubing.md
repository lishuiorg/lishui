According to the public notice of January 2024, Fermented Rice Pancake Making is one of the new inscriptions in the recommended list for the fourth batch of district-level intangible cultural heritage items. Its category is traditional craft and its applicant unit is the Jingqiao Town People's Government. The name is written with the characters for fermented glutinous rice and for a flat cake; the notice does not describe the ferment used or the form of the cake. This item, Lishui Rice Cake Making and Lishui Traditional Brewing were all submitted by the Jingqiao Town People's Government and all appear in the notice as food-related names.
