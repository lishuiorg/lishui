According to the public notice of January 2024, Hucun Rolling Dragon is an **extended project** in the recommended list for the fourth batch of district-level intangible cultural heritage items. Its category is traditional dance and its applicant unit is the Honglan Subdistrict Office. The item title carries the name Hucun, so the place of transmission is presumably in the Hucun area of Honglan Subdistrict.
