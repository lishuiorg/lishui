According to the public notice of January 2024, Fushan Small Peanut Roasting is one of the new inscriptions in the recommended list for the fourth batch of district-level intangible cultural heritage items. Its category is traditional craft and its applicant unit is the Baima Town People's Government. The first part of the title is the name of a mountain in Baima Town; the second part is a word describing the peanut variety or grade, which the notice does not explain. It is the only roasted-goods item among the projects recommended in this batch.
