According to the public notice of January 2024, Horn Lantern is one of the new inscriptions in the recommended list for the fourth batch of district-level intangible cultural heritage items. Its category is folk custom and its applicant unit is the Hefeng Town People's Government. The name is written with the characters for "horn" and "lantern"; the notice does not say whether "horn" refers to the shape of the lantern, to a musical instrument, or to the arrangement of a ceremonial procession, and the item is not placed in the traditional dance category.
