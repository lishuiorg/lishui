According to the public notice of January 2024, Mazhuang Dragon Lantern is an **extended project** in the recommended list for the fourth batch of district-level intangible cultural heritage items. Its category is traditional dance and its applicant unit is the Shiqiu Subdistrict Office. The item title carries the name Mazhuang, so the place of transmission is presumably in the Mazhuang area of Shiqiu Subdistrict.

> **Entry to be expanded.** This is a skeleton entry: at present it guarantees only that the fields and sources are complete.
