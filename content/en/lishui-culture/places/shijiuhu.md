## Geography and use

Shijiu Lake lies in the south-western part of Hefeng Town, Lishui District. The district government's Folk Customs page records that local people depend on Shijiu Lake for their living; the project page for Longwu (Luoshan Great Dragon) on the China Intangible Cultural Heritage Network records that Luoshan Village adjoins the lake and that in the winter dry season the lakebed becomes the ground for the dragon dance.

For the purposes of the culture site, this entry is concerned with the use of the lake as the dancing ground of the Luoshan Great Dragon. The lake's own geography, area and protected-area data are recorded by the entry on Shijiu Lake in the landscape site of this library; only material directly relevant to that use is cited here, with its sources named.

## Area and shoreline

Several official figures are given for the area of Shijiu Lake, and they are set side by side here without choosing between them:

- the "Geographical Position" page of the Lishui District People's Government website gives an area of the lake of **207.65 square kilometres**, of which **90.4 square kilometres** lie within Lishui District;
- the reply of the Nanjing Municipal People's Government of 5 December 2023 to Suggestion No. 0312 of the First Session of the Seventeenth Municipal People's Congress gives an area of the lake of **214.7 square kilometres**, of which 90.4 square kilometres lie within Lishui District;
- the reply of the Lishui District People's Government of 29 November 2023 to Suggestion No. 009 of the Second Session of the Seventeenth District People's Congress gives an area within Lishui of **91.2 square kilometres** and a shoreline length of **29.31 kilometres**, together with a total shoreline length of the lake of **70 kilometres**.

The three sets of figures each carry their own year and source. The two figures of 90.4 square kilometres for the area within Lishui District agree numerically, but one comes from the district government's "Geographical Position" page and the other from a municipal government reply, so they are still attributed separately. The two shoreline figures are not stated in the original as covering the whole lake or only the part within Lishui, and are recorded as written without being sorted out.

One easily confused figure must be kept distinct: the "Shijiu Lake basin of 599.39 square kilometres" on the district government's "Geographical Position" page is a **basin area**, not the area of the lake, and must not be used interchangeably with the 207.65 or 214.7 square kilometres above.

## Note on dating

None of the sources above records when the lake was formed or when it received its present name, so the era of this entry is given as "Date undetermined". The old gazetteers likewise record no date for its formation; volume 1 of the Kangxi Gazetteer of Lishui County, in the "Annals of the County", notes that in the first year of the Yongle reign a barrier was built at Guangtong Town and refers to "the waters of the Yanzhi River and Shijiu Lake", which shows the name of the lake was already known at that time, but the entry does not record a date of formation.

## Points of doubt

- The two figures for the area of the lake (207.65 and 214.7 square kilometres) and the three for the area within Lishui (90.4, 91.2 and 87.7 square kilometres) are set side by side and none is chosen for the others; the confidence of this entry is not raised on that account. For the detailed data and the sources item by item, see the entry on Shijiu Lake in the landscape site of this library.
- For the shoreline length, the two figures of 70 kilometres and 29.31 kilometres are not stated in the original as covering the whole lake or only the part within Lishui, and are not sorted out here.
- The date of the formation of Shijiu Lake, the date of its naming and water-level data are not recorded in the sources used.
- The relation between the waters of Shijiu Lake and the surrounding administrative areas, the towns, subdistricts and villages it spans, and the names used for it in earlier periods are not recorded in the sources used, apart from the three subdistricts, unnamed, mentioned in the reply above.
