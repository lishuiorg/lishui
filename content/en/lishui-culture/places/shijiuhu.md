## Geography and use

Shijiu Lake lies in the south-western part of Hefeng Town, Lishui District. The district government's Folk Customs page records that local people depend on Shijiu Lake for their living; the project page for Longwu (Luoshan Great Dragon) on the China Intangible Cultural Heritage Network records that Luoshan Village adjoins the lake and that in the winter dry season the lakebed becomes the ground for the dragon dance.

## Note on dating

Neither of the two sources used here records when the lake was formed or when it received its present name; the era is therefore given as "Date undetermined".

## Points of doubt

- Figures for the lake's area, shoreline length and water level have no source in this library and are not given.
- The relation between the lake and the surrounding administrative areas, and the names used for it in earlier periods, are not recorded in the sources used here.
