## Listing

In its reply of 28 November 2023, the Lishui District Culture and Tourism Bureau states that Tai Village in Jingqiao, Luoshan in Hefeng, Yangjia Village in Sunjiaxiang and two other villages were included in the sixth batch of traditional villages of Jiangsu Province. This follows the bureau's working account; the list is authoritative as published by the provincial housing department.

## Note on dating

The reply text for the listing gives no date of founding and no historical period for Tai Village, so the era of this entry is given as "Date undetermined".

## Points of doubt

- The formal publication of the sixth batch list could not be verified here; this entry rests on the reply of the district culture and tourism bureau.
- Tai Village is in Jingqiao Town. The early history of the village, its surviving buildings and the origin of its name are not recorded in the sources used here.
