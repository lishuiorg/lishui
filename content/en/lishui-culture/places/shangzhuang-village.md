## Listing

A report of 14 July 2025 by the district media centre states that the Jiangsu Provincial Department of Housing and Urban-Rural Development published a notice listing the eighth batch of traditional villages of Jiangsu Province, and that Shangzhuang in Shanggang Community, Honglan Subdistrict, Lishui District, was among those selected. The report is a news item; the list is authoritative as published by the provincial department.

## Note on dating

The notice text for the listing gives no date of founding and no historical period for Shangzhuang, so the era of this entry is given as "Date undetermined".

## Points of doubt

- The headline of the report says "two villages"; only the one listing for Shangzhuang in Shanggang Community, Honglan Subdistrict, could be verified here, and the other is not written up.
- Should the notice and the formally published list differ, the latter prevails.
- The early history of Shangzhuang and the state of its surviving buildings are not recorded in the sources used here.
